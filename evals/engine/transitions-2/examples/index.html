Three ways one chapter becomes the next

Each example runs through all eight chapters of a portfolio, for a made-up person. Every picture and word comes from that person's details, so the same example would go into anyone's portfolio with their own words and places. Switch person at the top of an example to see it.

Example A

One sheet

The person's CV is one sheet of paper. Scrolling bends it into each chapter's picture, and the next picture is printed on it as it turns.

  1. CV page
  2. wraps into a globe with the places
  3. unrolls into the diploma
  4. stretches into a timeline of jobs
  5. folds into a leaflet of skills
  6. splits into a wall of projects
  7. pairs into quote cards
  8. folds back into the CV, to download

Example B

One map

Every chapter stands where it happened, on one map of the person's life. The camera flies from place to place, and each chapter rises out of the map.

  1. a sign where they live now
  2. the route between their places
  3. the diploma rises at the school
  4. a building for each job, as tall as the years
  5. skills on the buildings that used them
  6. projects where they were made
  7. quotes where those people worked
  8. back home, with the whole life in view
Open
Example C

Pop-up book

Each chapter is a spread in a book: words on the left, a picture that stands up on the right. Turning the page folds one picture flat and raises the next.

  1. a cut-out of the person
  2. a map with flags at each place
  3. the school and the diploma
  4. a building for each job
  5. tabbed cards of skills
  6. project cards to open
  7. speech bubbles
  8. an envelope with the email
Open
