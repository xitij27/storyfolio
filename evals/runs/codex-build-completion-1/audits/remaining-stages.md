## Concrete design-to-rubric gap: wording choices

The design's **Ask or decide**, lines 138 to 140, says:

> For words it shows the sentences themselves: three, each as the whole
> beat would read, with room for the person to write their own.

The most direct reading is **three total presented versions**, with a
recommendation among them. It does not say three additional alternatives
besides the recommendation. This audit does not turn that into four.

The frozen [rubric](../inputs/rubric.md), line 39, instead asks for
“recommended opening/closing wording and meaningful alternatives”. It
specifies neither three versions nor full-beat presentation. The saved
[turn-1 reply](../embedded-completion-1/baseline-1/turns/01/reply.md),
lines 26 to 33, offers O1/O2 and C1/C2: two versions per placement, one
recommended and one alternative. The saved
[copy-choices file](../embedded-completion-1/baseline-1/turns/01/snapshot/files/docs/story/copy-choices.md),
lines 14 to 38, confirms those same pairs and describes where fixed
sentences follow or precede them. It does not show three complete versions
of either beat. Permission to send custom edits is present.

Thus a judge can legitimately pass the frozen approval criterion while
the fuller design requirement remains unmet. Keep that distinction in
the study findings. Do not retroactively tighten the frozen rubric,
change its raw score, send an extra paid corrective turn, or treat this
audit as authorization for a skill edit. This is a concrete omission in
the current measurement contract and a saved difference from the full
product design, not proof that the author approved removing that rule.
