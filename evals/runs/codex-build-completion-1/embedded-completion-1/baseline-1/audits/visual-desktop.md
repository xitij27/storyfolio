# Independent desktop visual review

11 October 2026. A coordinator reviewer opened all 14 original PNG files
individually with original-resolution display, not only contact sheets or
thumbnails. Each image is 1440 by 900 and shows Bench paper. Paths below are
relative to this audit. SHA-256 values identify the exact reviewed bytes.
No source, image, expectation or automatic report was changed. No model,
browser or build was started for this review.

No blocking layout or readability defect was observed in this set. All
seven beat headings and the eleven fixed sentences are readable across the
captures. The rail's active chapter agrees with each depicted beat,
including both Observation beats. The six-row object preserves contribution
and evidence distinctions; no contradictory factual claim was seen.

Texture labels remain softer than HTML text, especially the smaller
responsibility and transition cards, but their wording is legible here.
Small schematic previews are accompanied by explanatory HTML and expansion
links. Their internal fine print should be read in the linked full drawing.
The right-edge rail comes close to some card edges but does not cover card
wording in these captures.

## Limits

Six middle/end pairs are byte-identical: Scope, Responsibilities, State,
Transition, Observation fixture and Observation evidence. Every file was
opened, but repeated bytes do not establish additional visual variation or
continuous movement. Review's middle/end captures differ in vertical text
position. This audit does not infer animation quality from static images.

The [desktop report](../acceptance/result/desktop/report.json) separately
records an empty problems list. Its passing checks are not a substitute for
this pixel review, nor does this review establish all other conditions.
Only Bench paper at the two saved desktop positions per beat is covered
here. Other looks, fallback images, every intermediate scroll position,
frame rate, real devices and actual mail/CV actions need their own evidence.
The new direct fragment is not independently exercised by viewing these
scroll-sampled PNGs. Actual author review and physical-phone performance
remain pending. This artifact also supplies no substitute for the separate
judge call; a prompt-size stop cannot be described as a completed judgement.
