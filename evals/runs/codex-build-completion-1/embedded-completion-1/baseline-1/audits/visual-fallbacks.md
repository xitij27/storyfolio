# Independent acceptance fallback image review

11 October 2026. All 21 original PNGs below were opened individually with `view_image`. These are the final coordinator acceptance screenshots, not actor contact sheets. No source, actor output or acceptance result was edited.

The sampled fallback pages retain readable story copy, links and the explanatory transition/fixture diagrams. No blocking overlap or horizontal clipping was found in these 21 frames. No-WebGL and context-loss originals are 1440 by 900 pixels; no-JavaScript originals are 390 by 844 pixels. Each is a scroll-position screenshot, so adjacent sections can continue beyond the top or bottom.

One content limitation remains visible in all three state-section screenshots: the sentence about six narrative rows has no corresponding rows or table in the static page. The fallback preserves the prose but does not expose that 3D formation. This is a specific wording/representation gap, not evidence that the whole fallback is unreadable. No fix was made during this baseline review.

Static screenshots cannot independently prove JavaScript disabling, a context-loss event, keyboard operation, link destinations, full-scroll coverage or physical-phone behavior. Those behavior claims require the accompanying checker evidence. These screenshots do not establish author approval or the quality of the animated 3D presentation.
