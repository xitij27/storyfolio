# Storyboard — Rafael Costa

Concept: **The Weekly Report.** The question: *What's worth doing again every Monday?*
The object: a pad of 8 report sheets — Atlantico, DataTugas, Helix, FraudShield, the Health Data Hackathon, Lisbon Rent Map, Meridian, AutoPilot — the same 8 pieces throughout, never swapped for others.

## Someone with thirty seconds

Beat 1.1 alone carries his name, what he does, and one proof figure (the Helix five-hours-to-two cut), plus the question. A nav rail, visible from the first screen, jumps straight to any chapter by name. A "Contact" link in the same rail skips straight to chapter 7's email and CV links — so the name, the work, the proof and the way to reach him are all available without a single scroll.

## The look and the type

**Printed Monday** (the one I'd start from) — ground: warm paper white (#F5F1E8). Ink: near-black charcoal (#1B1B18). One accent: a stamp red (#C14C32), used only for the pinned note and for links. Sheet surface: faintly textured off-white paper with a thin ruled line, soft stacked-paper shadow.

**Night shift** — ground: near-black (#0E1116). Ink: cool off-white (#E8EAED). One accent: a dashboard teal (#3DDC97), used for figures and links. Sheet surface: dark graphite cards with hairline borders, as if lit from a monitor rather than a desk lamp.

**Ledger** — ground: gray-beige (#EDEBE6). Ink: navy (#1F2B45). One accent: a date-stamp amber (#D98E34). Sheet surface: cream paper over a faint grid, closer to a bank's own paperwork.

Two typefaces: a clean grotesk sans for the running text (captions, nav, the close), and a monospace, typewriter-styled face for anything printed on a sheet's face — headers, figures, dates — since a report is typed, not set.

He picks between the three looks by scrolling through a short sample, once there's something built to scroll.
