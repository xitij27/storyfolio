I've worked out three concepts that differ in form and object, not just colour. Full writeups are in `docs/story/concepts.md` — here they are in short:

**1. The Ferment Log** — question: *"What happens to what's left over?"* Form is the ferment log (started / salted / checked / ready) — the document you named first. Object is a row of jars, the shape of your own jar wall. Opens on scraps and salt in the dark; the jars assemble into a timeline of your jobs, then into the actual jar wall grid carrying real photos; close on the wall at rest with Callum's line on one jar.

**2. The Prep List** — question: *"How does a kitchen get ready before anyone orders?"* Form is the prep list, object is a rail of kitchen tickets that arrange into your stations (larder/garnish → sauce → stage → pass → head chef), then flip into a photo wall. Close is a full ticket rail, ready for service.

**3. An Issue of Brine** — question: *"What's worth keeping?"* Form is a zine spread, object is flat pages laid out two-up. Problem: this one leans on Brine as a whole object, and Olu draws for Brine — even without showing his drawings, using the zine itself as the page's skeleton risks implying his hand is in it. Needs either his yes or a clear call with you to keep the pages text-and-photo only.

**I'd choose Concept 1.** It's the only one where the form and the object are both things you named yourself, unprompted, as what you actually work from. Its question legitimately covers the waste cut, the supplier cut, Sunday Pot, and Brine without stretching the facts. And it puts the jar wall front and centre — the thing people actually stop you to ask about — rather than spending its best space on the pass (Concept 2) or depending on Olu's unresolved yes (Concept 3).
