# Three concepts — Ines Okafor

## Concept 1: The Ferment Log

**The question:** What do you do with what's left over?

**The form — the ferment log, as she keeps it (date started, salt, days checked, when ready):**

| Chapter | Role | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Started | The opening | Her name, the question |
| Logged | Who | Her own words — fermentation, whole-vegetable cooking, apprentices; her grandmother, who never measured |
| Dated | Where from | Leeds Kitchen College, Brassica, the Fjaerland stage, Corn Exchange, Saltmarsh — with years |
| Salted | How | The ferment log's own fields; the prep list; the menu written last, not first |
| Checked | What | The larder, the jar wall, the koji cupboard, the apprentice programme, the workshops — some shown, then all |
| Ready | What came of it | 14 kg to 5 kg of waste a week (the kitchen's, weighed over a month in 2023); 31 suppliers to 12; two apprentices a year since 2023 |
| Tasted | What others say | Callum Reyes's line about bins and a larder |
| Opened | The close | The answer, and how to reach her — apply, book a workshop, write |

**The object:** seven to nine plain glass jars, same size, same shape, a blank label on each face. Standing together at rest, they are the jar wall.

**What it becomes, in three chapters:**
- *Dated:* the jars space out along a line, one per place and year, like a row set down a shelf in the order they were filled.
- *Checked:* the jars close into the grid of the jar wall itself. Each face carries a real photograph — hers from the larder, Priya's from the restaurant, credited — and one jar holds the koji room, described in her own words as "a cupboard."
- *Ready:* the jars tip, and each label becomes a figure: the waste number, the supplier number, the apprentices.

**The opening and the close:** it opens on one unlabelled jar and the question beneath it. It closes on the full wall again, every jar labelled, the answer given beside a line for how to reach her.

**What it stands on:** the larder (40+ ferments on rotation), the jar wall, the koji room, the waste figure (confirmed: the kitchen's, prep and plate only, weighed over a month in 2023), the supplier figure, the apprentice figure, her grandmother, Callum's quote, Priya's photographs (credited), her own larder photographs.

**What it needs from her:** the photo files themselves — hers, and Priya's with credit. Nothing outstanding.

**Why it's hers:** the jar wall isn't fermentation's emblem — it's the specific wall in her specific restaurant that strangers already stop and ask her about, built from what her kitchen used to throw away.

## Concept 2: The Prep List

**The question:** What has to happen before anything happens?

**The form — the prep list, which she said runs longer than the menu:**

| Chapter | Role | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| To do | The opening | Her name, the question |
| Mise | Who | "Most of what I care about happens before service" |
| Started | Where from | College, Brassica, the Fjaerland stage, Corn Exchange — with years |
| Method | How | The ferment log's fields; the prep list; the menu last, not first |
| In progress | What | The apprentice programme, the workshops, the larder being built |
| Done | What came of it | The waste, supplier and apprentice figures |
| Said | What others say | Callum's line |
| Service | The close | The answer, and how to reach her |

**The object:** six to ten small, identical prep tins, each with a label slot. At rest, lined along a counter edge, as they'd sit before service.

**What it becomes, in three chapters:**
- *Started:* the tins space into a line of places and years, like stations set along a pass.
- *In progress:* the tins open and nest into the larder's shape, each showing one real photograph or one fact about an apprentice's or a workshop's day.
- *Done:* the tins stack into a tower of the three figures.

**The opening and the close:** opens on one empty tin on a bare counter. Closes with every tin filled and lined up ready, the answer given, and how to reach her.

**What it stands on:** her own line about what happens before service, the prep list, the ferment log, the apprentice and workshop facts, the three figures, Callum's quote.

**What it needs from her:** the same photographs as Concept 1. Nothing outstanding.

**Why it's hers:** a prep list that outruns the menu is a specific, odd fact she said about her own kitchen — most chef sites lead with the plate; hers would lead with the list that makes the plate possible.

## Concept 3: Brine

**The question:** What's worth writing down?

**The form — Brine, her own zine, four issues, 2022–2025:**

| Chapter | Role | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Cover | The opening | Her name, the question |
| Note | Who | Her own words from the About section |
| Masthead | Where from | College, Brassica, the Fjaerland stage, Corn Exchange, Saltmarsh |
| Process | How | The ferment log, the prep list, the menu last |
| Feature | What | The larder, the jar wall, the koji room, the apprentices, the workshops |
| Field notes | What came of it | The three figures |
| Letters | What others say | Callum's line |
| Back page | The close | The answer, and how to reach her |

**The object:** seven or eight flat page-shapes, which can fan, hang or fold. At rest they sit as one stacked zine.

**What it becomes, in three chapters:**
- *Masthead:* the pages fan into a timeline, one per place and year.
- *Feature:* the pages hang in a line, each carrying a real photograph of hers or Priya's, credited. (Not Olu's drawings — see below.)
- *Field notes:* the pages fold down into three cards, one per figure.

**The opening and the close:** opens on the cover of the first issue, blank until the question sets across it. Closes with the pages gathered back into one whole zine, the answer on the back page, with how to reach her.

**What it stands on:** the four real issues of Brine, her About section, the larder and jar-wall facts, the three figures, Callum's quote.

**What it needs from her:** Olu's yes before any of his drawings from Brine appear — she said she'll ask him first, so this concept either waits on that answer or opens without his pages, using only her own and Priya's photographs in the meantime. A scan of an actual Brine page or cover would also strengthen it.

**Why it's hers:** she is the only one of the three with something already printed — four real issues are proof she already turns her work into something kept and handed to someone else, before any site does it for her.

## Which I would choose, and why

**Concept 1, the Ferment Log.** Picked by Ines, 2026-10-10. It needs nothing still outstanding (unlike Brine, which waits on Olu), and its object — the jar wall — is the one piece of her own restaurant that strangers already stop and ask her about, not a stand-in for "chef" in general. Its question, *what do you do with what's left over*, is the one thread that actually runs under everything in the fact sheet: the larder built from trim and whey, Callum's line about bins, Sunday Pot's food that restaurants would otherwise throw out, and Brine itself, a zine about preserving. The Prep List is a close second and could replace it if she'd rather lead with how she works than with what the kitchen keeps.
