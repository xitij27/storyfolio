## Someone with thirty seconds

First screen (1.1–1.2) carries her name, what she does (second-year mechanical engineering, chasing a placement), and the question. A thin rail, present from chapter 2 on, jumps straight to any chapter by name. One link in the rail, "Get in touch," skips straight to chapter 8. A visitor who clicks that from screen one reaches her email and CV in one jump, having seen her name and the question on the way.

The one proof a thirty-second visitor gets without digging: the bracket's 18% figure, visible in the rail's "Part: the bracket" label, and reachable in one click.

## The look and the type

Three options, to pick by scrolling once built:

1. **Workshop grey.** Ground: concrete grey. Ink: near-black. Accent: inspection-tag orange. Pieces: brushed aluminium, with a pale blue tint on the bracket's still-simulated surface to mark it apart from the made parts.
2. **Garage floor.** Ground: warm, oil-stained concrete. Ink: charcoal. Accent: faded safety yellow. Pieces: raw steel with real rust and patina on the bike parts, clean aluminium on the bracket and charger — the finish itself tells old from new.
3. **Logbook ink.** Ground: pale, page-like cream. Ink: blue-black. Accent: correction-pen red, used only on the verdict tags. Pieces rendered as line drawings — white-on-dark blueprint strokes — rather than photoreal surfaces.

Two typefaces: one plain grotesk for the running captions, one stencilled/stamped face for whatever is printed on a tag — the one piece of lettering that belongs to the object, not the page.
