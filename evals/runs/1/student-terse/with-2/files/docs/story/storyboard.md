# Storyboard — Mina Park, "The Job Card"

Question: **How do you know a fix actually worked?**

Object: 7 workshop tags — the rectangular card kind tied to a bike with string — each carrying one real piece of Mina's work on its face:

| Tag | Carries |
|---|---|
| T1 | The 1984 frame — bought for thirty pounds, with a flat tyre |
| T2 | The rear wheel — the cracked hub flange, hiding under the grease |
| T3 | The bottom bracket — the seized cup |
| T4 | The paint — forty years of it, stripped |
| T5 | The pedal-box bracket — Hallam Racing, CAD + simulation |
| T6 | The hand-crank charger — gear train and housing, group of four |
| T7 | A tag left blank — the one that isn't answered yet |

## Someone with thirty seconds

The first screen (beat 1.1–1.2) carries her name, her year and field, and one proof: the bracket's 18% figure. A rail on the side lists all 7 chapters by name and jumps to any of them. A "Contact" link in the rail skips straight to the sign-off.

## Without the scene

| Where | What the visitor gets |
|---|---|
| On a phone | The same 7 chapters, stacked vertically. Tags lie in a simple vertical list instead of a fan or a ring; flips become a tap, not a scroll-held gesture. |
| With reduced motion | No scroll-driven movement. Each chapter appears as one settled state; flips and the fan happen as an instant tap, not an animation. |
| With no WebGL | The 3D tags are replaced by flat styled card images in the same layout, same photos, same captions. |
| With no JavaScript | One static HTML page, the 7 chapters as plain sections top to bottom, tag art as plain `<img>` tags, email and CV links as ordinary links. |

## The look and the type

Three looks, each named for its ground, ink, one accent, and the tag surface:

1. **Workshop kraft** — ground: warm kraft-tag tan. Ink: charcoal. Accent: grease-pencil orange (used for the "TESTED" stamps). Tags: lightly textured cardstock, a punched hole, a loop of string.
2. **Shop-floor dark** — ground: oiled-concrete grey. Ink: chalk white. Accent: safety orange. Tags: dark board, marker-style lettering, as on a bike shop's price board.
3. **Clean service desk** — ground: off-white. Ink: navy. Accent: a single stamp-ink red. Tags: crisp white card, as a modern shop's printed ticket.

Mina picks by scrolling, once it's built.

Two typefaces: a plain humanist sans for the running text, and a stamped/typewriter-style face for anything printed on the tags themselves, since job cards are filled in by hand or by stamp, not typeset.
