## Someone with thirty seconds

The sticky header gives the first three without any scroll: her name and both titles, and a contact link. The first screen (Open, beat 1) gives the fourth: the proof strip, "71% to 94% in nine months," sitting under the still wheel. The rail in the header jumps to any chapter; its contact link skips straight to the close.

## Without the scene

| Where | What the visitor gets |
|---|---|
| On a phone | The wheel becomes four stacked cards, Plan/Do/Study/Act, in page order, each filling in as it's reached. Hover becomes tap: tapping a tool icon, a workaround panel, or a ward mark opens it in place. The header collapses to a thin bar with the name, a menu icon for the rail, and the contact link. |
| With reduced motion | No turning, no counting up. Each beat appears already in its settled state as it's scrolled to — the 94 is just there, not counted to. The wedges fill in one flat step, not a sweep. |
| With no WebGL | The wheel is drawn flat, in CSS or SVG: four coloured quarters in a ring, same labels, same numbers, same click targets. Nothing shown here depends on 3D to be understood. |
| With no JavaScript | A plain HTML page, chapters in order top to bottom, same captions, same figures, the blank handover sheet and the workaround panels as ordinary images and text. The rail is a set of anchor links. Nothing in the page's meaning is only in the scene — the text carries the full story on its own. |

## The look and the type

Three directions, from the wheel's own material. She picks once it's built and she can scroll it.

- **Ward noticeboard.** Ground: matte off-white, like poster card. Ink: charcoal. One accent: the red used for an early-warning score that's tripped — kept for the proof numbers and nothing else. Pieces: flat, printed-looking, like a QI poster pinned to cork.
- **Night shift.** Ground: near-black navy, the ward at 3 a.m. Ink: warm off-white. One accent: a cool monitor-trace teal, for the active wedge and the climbing number. Pieces: a faint glass sheen, softly lit from inside.
- **Day shift.** Ground: warm kraft tan, like a clipboard. Ink: ballpoint blue. One accent: a muted clinical green, saved for "94" and nothing before it. Pieces: a slightly worn paper texture.

Two typefaces. One for the running text: a plain, humanist sans, built to be read at length, not a display face. One for what's printed on the wheel itself — the labels, the numbers, the wedge names — a condensed, slightly institutional sans, the kind printed on a hospital form or a wristband, used nowhere else on the page.
