## The poster (the fullest story in the material)
"Why nurses skip the scan: an observational study of workarounds on one cardiac ward" — A. Brennan, RGN. Irish Nursing Informatics Conference 2025 (poster). Single author. (poster-abstract.md)
- Background: 29% of doses were given without a scan on her ward.
- Method: 40 hours of observation across day and night shifts; short interviews with 22 nurses.
- Findings: three workarounds caused most missed scans — wristbands that wouldn't scan after a shower, scanners kept on a trolley away from the medication room, scanning from a printed sheet of barcodes kept in a pocket. Each saved a nurse 1–4 minutes a round. "None was laziness."
- What changed: scanners fixed beside the medication room door, wristband stock changed, printed sheets stopped.
- Result: scanning rose to 94% in nine months — this is the same figure as the LinkedIn bullet, so the two sources agree here.
