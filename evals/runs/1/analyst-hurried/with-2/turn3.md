## Phone, and without the 3D
Same six chapters, same order, same words, always — the text is ordinary HTML over the scene, never scroll-only.
- **Phone:** the node graph becomes a flat vertical connected-dot timeline, single column, larger tap targets, scroll-triggered fades instead of camera moves.
- **Reduced motion:** no camera movement or parallax — each chapter settles in with a plain crossfade.
- **No WebGL:** the same flat version phones get — an SVG/CSS pipeline diagram standing in for the 3D blocks.
- **No JavaScript:** a plain HTML page, six headed sections in order, static illustration instead of the graph, no animation — everything still readable.
