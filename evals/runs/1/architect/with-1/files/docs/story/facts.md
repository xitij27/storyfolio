5. **A School That Grows:** a team of four. She did the structure and the sections. The six boards are team work; her specific contribution is the structural scheme and the sections.
6. **Tom Reid's photographs (Calder Court, Kirk Street):** belong to the practice and to him. She would need to ask both, and credit him if used. **Not cleared yet.**
7. **Practice drawings (Calder Court, Kirk Street):** the practice's property. She can probably show the detail she drew herself, but has to ask first. **Not cleared yet.**
8. **Cabin at Kashuby:** all hers — design, drawings, photographs, build. Fully clear to use. She did not separately confirm whether her father may be named or shown in photographs — see `decided-for-you.md`.
9. **Kirk Street outcome figure:** none given. "Church hall into six flats" appears to be the whole stated result.
10. **Audience and ending:** in two years she wants her own practice. She wants the site found by (a) people with an old house or a small plot, and (b) schools that need a guest critic. This defines the site's purpose and its closing move.
11. **What she works from:** the drawing set, in its fixed order — location, plans, sections, elevations, details, schedules — and the RIBA Plan of Work stages, 0 to 7. On site: the snag list.
12. **What she learned from:** her father's workshop, and a book of Swiss timber joints she has owned since she was nineteen.
