| Where | What you get |

| Reduced motion | No continuous or scroll-scrubbed movement. Each beat appears as its settled state and crossfades to the next. Same content, fixed gentle pace. |
| No WebGL | Every 3D beat replaced by a flat photo or line drawing of that beat's settled state — e.g. the joint's drawing becomes a plain photograph with the same caption. All text stays, because text is ordinary HTML over the scene throughout. |
| No JavaScript | A plain long page: your name, the question, the six chapters' text and photos top to bottom, ending with contact. Nothing missing that the other rows have. |

## Look and type

Object's material is structural timber, so the look should feel like workshop and drawing board, not screen-gloss. Three candidates — you pick once there's something to scroll through:

- **A, Drawing board** — cream ground, graphite ink, one terracotta-red accent, pale timber with fine etched linework.
- **B, Night site** — charcoal ground, warm off-white ink, amber work-light accent, dark-stained timber lit like scaffolding at dusk.
- **C, Lime and linen** — pale limewash grey, deep slate-blue ink, a muted moss-green accent (echoing Calder Court's winter gardens), raw spruce with pencil-thin annotations.

Two typefaces throughout: a plain humanist sans for all body text, and a technical, hand-drawn-sheet lettering face for anything printed directly on the timber — labels, the title block, your name on the ridge beam.
