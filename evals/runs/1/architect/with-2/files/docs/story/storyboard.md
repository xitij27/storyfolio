# Storyboard — Marta Kowalczyk

Concept: **the snag list** (`docs/story/concepts.md`, Concept 3, chosen).

Question: **What's worth fixing, and how do you know it's fixed?**

Object: five snag tags — small numbered tabs on string, the kind tied to a fault on site. One tag per thing she does: Calder Court, Kirk Street, A School That Grows, the Cabin at Kashuby, and her teaching at Clyde School. At rest they hang, loose, off the plain outline of an ordinary building.

## The look and the type

Three looks, each named by the object's own material. Pick by scrolling, once it's built.

**A. Site.** Ground: concrete grey. Ink: near-black. Accent: the orange of snag-tag string. Pieces: matte kraft card, slightly worn, numbers stamped as if stencilled on site.

**B. Drawing.** Ground: cream, like tracing paper. Ink: graphite. Accent: a single snag-red. Pieces: thin card with fine linework on their faces, as if drawn rather than printed.

**C. Timber.** Ground: pale ply. Ink: charcoal. Accent: the orange of builder's twine. Pieces: thin plywood, numbers burned in rather than printed — closest to the cabin's own material.

Two typefaces: a plain humanist sans for all reading text, and a stencilled, site-stamp face for whatever's printed on the tags themselves — the only place the form's own voice appears in the type.
