# Decided without asking

| Decision | Why | How to change it |
|---|---|---|
| Object is a wall of jars, not a chef's hat or a knife | Jars are what's in Ines's own photographs, and what the ferment log is kept on; a hat or knife is the trade's emblem, not hers | Tell me and I'll re-run the object search |
| Six chapters: Started, Salted, Checked day by day, What's on the shelf, Tasted, Ready | These are the ferment log's own fields, in the order a jar moves through them, with "Tasted" and "Ready" split out so there's room for the quote and the outcomes | Say which to merge or split |
| A fixed bar runs the whole page, carrying her name, title and a rail | Needed so a visitor with thirty seconds gets the name and what she does without scrolling | Drop it and the thirty-second rule fails; tell me if you want it to look different |
| No page for each project; Sunday Pot and Brine sit inside chapter 6 | Three things to show (the larder, Sunday Pot, Brine) don't need three more pages; folding them into the close keeps the story one piece | Ask and I'll give any of the three its own stop |
| No blog | Brine already does this job | Tell me if you want one anyway |
| No chat, no languages | Nothing in the material calls for either | Tell me if that's wrong |
| No legal notice | The site is planned to collect nothing — a mailto-style link, not a form | If you want a contact form that stores entries, this needs revisiting |
| 404 page is one empty, unlabelled jar | Keeps the object in frame even off the happy path | Tell me if you'd rather something plainer |
| Two looks offered, "Larder" and "Pantry at night" | Both come from the jars' own material — linen and glass, day and night in the larder | Pick one, or ask for a third |
| Two typefaces: one for reading, one hand-stencilled for jar labels | The form (the ferment log) is handwritten; the label face should look like what's actually marker-on-glass in her kitchen | Say if you'd rather one face throughout |
