# Storyboard — Ines Okafor

Concept: **The ferment log, kept in jars** (`docs/story/concepts.md`). Question: *What are you doing while it waits?*
One object throughout: a jar — a plain cylinder with a label plane. Eight jars, one per facet of her work, used in every chapter, rearranged chapter to chapter, whole again at the close.

## Someone with thirty seconds

The first screen (beat 1.1) carries her name, her title and place ("Head Chef, Saltmarsh, Leeds"), and one proof — Callum's line is held back for chapter 7, so the proof on screen one is the jar wall thumbnail itself, visible before any scroll. A rail down the side names all eight chapters and jumps to any of them. One link, visible throughout, skips straight to "Ready to serve" — the close and the contact line.

## The look and the type

Three options, each as ground / ink / accent / surface of the jars. Picked by scrolling through, once built.

1. **Larder** — ground: warm plaster off-white. Ink: charcoal near-black. Accent: a turmeric gold. Jars: pale stoneware, hand-labels.
2. **Counter at night** — ground: near-black slate. Ink: warm cream. Accent: amber, as if lit from inside the jars. Jars: dark glass.
3. **Paper label** — ground: unbleached kraft. Ink: dark brown. Accent: a single stamped red. Jars: clear glass, so the ferment's own colour carries the accent.

Two typefaces: one plain, readable face for the running text; one label face — a stencil or grease-pencil-style letterform — used only on the jars themselves, for dates, places and numbers.
