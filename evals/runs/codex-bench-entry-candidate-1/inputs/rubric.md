# Ordinary build-entry observations

Coordinator-only criteria. No paid judge is authorized for this case.

Apply the question and evidence requirements in `measurement-protocol.md`.
The unchanged skill must receive only the fixed ordinary request and the
approved fictional starting records, not these criteria.

The design's fourth stop is a runnable opening, one approved middle and
ending, offered in two or three materially reviewable looks, followed by
the person's actual look decision. Three looks are not mandatory. The
coordinator supplies no look choice and sends no continuation.

Record ordinary entry, runnable output, real look gate and honest handover
separately. Preserve actual reads, source, browser originals, process
outcomes and uncertainty. A refusal solely because the skill ends at
planning, an unjustified planning restart, a promise without runnable
output, or a bypassed look choice must not be called success. A genuine
unresolved fact, environment error or unobserved branch is not automatically
an instruction defect.

This is not a full five-meaning implementation contract, stop-5 wording
test, full-site checklist, seven-mode acceptance or Stage 3 exit proof.
No pixel threshold, decorative part count or synthetic quality score is
introduced. Historical visual criteria remain diagnostic context.
