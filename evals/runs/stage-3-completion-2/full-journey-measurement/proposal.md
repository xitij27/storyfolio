# Proposed independent full-journey measurement

Source-only proposal for root review, before the `full-journey-1` baseline.
No helper, tests, build, browser or model is launched by this document.
Proposed implementation lives beside it, outside actor inputs, and all code
and transitive imports are added to the cohort freeze before the actor.
The [journey protocol](../../../builds/full-journey-1/protocol.md) and
[criteria](../../../builds/full-journey-1/criteria.md) remain the authority.

## Three different observations

| Gate | Browser evidence | Reuse and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Stop 2 | All advertised concepts, each actual route and three actual beats on desktop and phone; any real common chooser by pointer and sequential keyboard | Small adaptation of the narrow entry worker to per-concept routes and beat IDs. Do not call concepts appearance looks or assume shared IDs. Source and originals must demonstrate three materially distinct runnable concepts, not three tokens. |
| Stop 4 | Every advertised appearance of the approved opening/middle/ending on desktop and phone, with actual switches | Reuse the existing narrow worker and the frozen semantic target protocol. No full-site or seven-mode pass is inferred. Missing/excess/broken looks stay observed, not selected away. |
| Stop 6 | Every final beat, desktop/phone/short height, reduced motion, no WebGL, no JavaScript and actual context loss; both directions of every animated transition and ordinary controls | Study-local single-mode derivative of the existing capture path using its trusted command, geometry, travel and control functions. A separate complete-site aggregate requires every required mode and final content/asset check. |

Desktop is 1440x900, phone 390x844 and short height 1280x720, preserving
the established conditions. The six approved visual criteria remain
Backdrop, Depth, Swap, Detail/hierarchy, Spatial and Phone/still. A command
or hash comparison cannot pass those criteria or real author acceptance.

## Why an unchanged CLI is insufficient

`evals/check-build.mjs` always runs its whole matrix. Its schema 2 validator
requires the five specific depth-study IDs. Schema 1 omits the deeper
travel/control capture and short-height mode. Neither can truthfully pass
the actor's unknown 12 to 22 final beats unchanged. The proposal therefore
does not forge depth IDs, weaken that validator, or claim its old contract
passed. Historical checker and results stay untouched.

Use the already generic `diagnosticPlan`, `captureTravel`, `inventoryControls`
and `exerciseControl` primitives from `build-capture.mjs`. Their observations
are useful for arbitrary observed ordered IDs. A narrow journey assessment
uses actual final coverage, never the old error wording about five beats.
Reuse `fixedCommands`, runtime identity and request-failure checks from
`build-check.mjs`; do not import actor check scripts as expected truth.
The new code owns only stage-target validation, single-mode orchestration,
source/build binding and aggregate coverage. No shared runner or engine
change is needed for this measurement design.

## Prospective rules, post-turn observed identities

Root saves source-bound observed targets after each closed, completed turn
and before measurement. Bind cohort/input digest, exact turn result, final
snapshot, original full source inventory, discovery files and review author.
The frozen schema/rules precede the actor; actual routes, names, IDs and
selectors do not. No apparatus, formation or hidden expected answer is added.

Stop 2 records every public concept, its three source-defined beat IDs and
meaning, route, aliases and actual chooser when present. An absent chooser
is not invented if the three real routes are independently accessible; a
claimed but broken chooser is not waived. More/fewer than three alternatives
fail that requirement; discovery still records all of them.

Stop 4 records all public looks and the approved three-point semantic mapping.
Stop 6 maps the approved storyboard/checklist and selected look/wording onto
the complete actual route/beat inventory. Missing approved content remains
missing, while extra actual beats stay in the capture set. Do not derive
expected completeness solely from whatever the actor happened to implement.
Root records the recommendation and real gate/selection provenance separately.

Known focus-only skip navigation needs a frozen semantic allowance and an
exact source-bound declaration before browser observation. The narrow
allowance is only a document-start skip to the main story, exercised through
real Tab focus and target response. It is not a new exception for hidden,
disabled or inaccessible ordinary controls. Capture a raw control census
alongside the existing actionable inventory so filtered or unexercised
controls remain visible for source/intent review, not silently certified.

No authored output, a missing final handoff, refusal or a genuine unresolved
question produces an explicit non-passing gate, not a stock-starter pass.
An authored but apparently broken app still receives its one eligible build
attempt once integrity permits it. No next fixed actor prompt is sent just
because a measurement command completed.

## Proposed finite execution units

Root should approve these bounds before implementation/freeze:

- Stop 2: one build/browser gate attempt, at most 15 minutes including
  orderly shutdown. Queue every advertised concept and chooser. If its
  bound prevents full coverage, retain partial originals and block the gate.
- Stop 4: the existing one 15-minute narrow capture, no retry.
- Stop 6: one trusted type-generation/typecheck/lint/build phase, at most
  5 minutes including shutdown; then seven separately invoked single-mode
  captures, at most 8 minutes each including shutdown. One additional
  5-minute auxiliary-page/link/asset capture covers the project page,
  corrected CV download, navigation, icon/share resources and missing page.
  This is a finite maximum of 66 minutes of child lifetimes, not paid time
  or a hard whole-orchestration wall cap. Root can select tighter values
  before freeze, not after an inconvenient failure.

One new directory and terminal process record per planned unit; no automatic
retry or second attempt for a failed mode. Sequential modes are distinct
coverage, not repeated attempts at one mode. Failed build prevents browser
work. A timeout, external signal or inability to establish source/process
closure blocks subsequent units until diagnosed; it cannot be turned into
an accepted missing mode. Technical/visual failures inside a cleanly closed
unit may leave the remaining predeclared modes useful for diagnosis.

Separate mode processes reduce a single unit's duration. This does not
identify or claim to fix the earlier unknown SIGTERM source. With 22 beats
and many controls, a mode may exceed eight minutes; that is explicitly an
incomplete mode, not permission to omit beats or silently extend the bound.
If root instead wants phase/chunk scheduling, that rule must be designed
and frozen now. The initial recommendation favors seven simple mode units
over a broad dynamic scheduler.

## Source, build and artifact identity

Use the approved clean saved-source reconstruction method for each measured
gate. Restore only the exact settled snapshot, with no application repair.
Before excluding any captured extra, require root's exact metadata review;
omitted app source or unexplained entries block reconstruction. Preserve
the original workspace, actor artifacts and every old source-scope failure.

Stop 6 runs the trusted build only once. Save its source-before/after,
command and dependency-entrypoint identities, generated-config hashes,
build ID and generated build artifact hashes needed by the server. Each
mode starts its own loopback server from that same verified build, not an
actor-selected existing server. Assert unchanged application source before
and after every unit and exact build identity before later units; distinguish
runtime cache writes from immutable build files explicitly. Do not silently
rebuild when identity fails. Node/environment/browser resources and trusted
dependency scope are frozen; entrypoint hashes do not prove all transitive
dependency bytes. The original lockfile/install audit stays separate.

Keep raw responses, rendered HTML, full reports, warnings/errors/requests,
rest and moving originals, reduced-motion before/after pairs, actual
context-loss evidence and pointer/keyboard control trials. On animated
desktop/phone/short modes, retain three genuine moving samples for every
adjacent interval in both directions. Reduced motion requires actual
readable object/fallback evidence, not merely matching blank image hashes.
Context loss must be induced on a running context, then checked at every
beat; unsupported loss is unobserved, never a pass.

Every emitted PNG/JPEG original gets individual original-detail review and
exact path/hash/size coverage. The aggregate verifier rejects duplicate or
missing paths, source drift, altered raw artifacts, incomplete mode sets
and mismatched gate identities. Completeness, technical findings, source/
factual checks, visual findings and actor lifecycle remain separate fields.
External email delivery, publication, legal compliance and physical-phone
approval remain outside automated acceptance. Actual author desktop/phone
review follows, never simulated by this measurement.

## Review decision requested

Approve the study-local location, generic single-mode derivative and the
finite unit bounds, or choose smaller explicit scope before code is built.
Then implement only that surface with deterministic target/coverage/binding
tests and a separately labelled local no-model calibration, never borrowing
an evaluated actor for calibration. Freeze all resulting transitive inputs
before the full-journey baseline. No implementation is authorized merely
by this proposal being present.
