# Candidate 2, minimal later measurement adaptation

12 October 2026. Source-only proposal following the completed concept
replacement. This does not approve a later gate or launch. It refines the
[earlier seam inventory](candidate-2-later-measurement-plan.md) without
changing frozen helpers, policy, actor source, prompts or the six criteria.

## Implementation surface

Use a new study-local `full-journey-recovery/continuation-measurement/`
label. Prepare only the binding, reconstruction, one-unit wrapper, verifier
and stop-4 worker when an actual completed stop-4 handoff exists. Defer the
final-worker derivative until an actual eligible stop-6 output. Import the
existing assessment, controls, cache-identity and path helpers unchanged;
do not copy or relax their rules. A supplementary exact helper/import map
binds the new files alongside the original frozen inputs and amendment.
No new shared-runner feature or further recovery amendment is necessary
if the genuine new complete snapshot and app root validate.

1. **Binding adapter.** Start with
   `loadAmendment(path, {liveOriginal:false})`, preserving its original
   record. Pass that object plus `caseSource: validateCase(frozenCase)` to
   the frozen `readContinuationHistory`, before constructing the separate
   measurement record. This validates the two original turns and genuine
   append-only turns, session, budgets, prompt bytes and every artifact.
   For launch, require selected turn 4 or 6 to be the latest complete turn.
   Bind its actual `result.json`, `snapshot/manifest.json`,
   `snapshot/source.json` and complete `snapshot/files/` inventory. No
   182-file assumption or original recovery source substitution is allowed.
2. **Outer lifecycle adapter.** Reuse `assertSupervisor` for the actual
   completed recorder, raw streams/journal and bare terminal record. Replace
   only the old binding's `evals/codex-build-run.mjs` argv assertions with
   the frozen amended CLI, amendment/gate hashes and selected turn. Require
   normal complete closure, exact environment and no later actor while
   measurement is live. Historical audits may validate saved closure after
   the actor advances; they must not pretend the old source is still live.
3. **Reconstruction and wrapper.** Adapt the recovered `prepare-source`
   to copy the selected genuine snapshot, not `recoveryFilesRoot`. Before
   and after copying, use the runner's strict complete-source comparison,
   including sibling files and unknown-path checks. Reuse `resolveApplication`
   and `assertInstall` for the source-bound `site` package/lock and one
   recorded offline install in that application cwd. Keep the whole-workspace
   source comparison and protected-output exclusions. Refuse existing
   attempt/output directories. The wrapper loads this new binding and
   invokes the new worker; it otherwise retains logging, closure, bounds
   and the sequential no-retry schedule.
4. **Verifier adapter.** Reuse `assertUnitCoverage`, `assertCoverage`,
   `assessGateReport`, `assessMode` and policy byte-for-byte. Audit the new
   binding and real invocation identities, raw lifecycle/streams, complete
   artifact inventory and every actually reviewed original. Recompute saved
   technical assessments from reports; no trusted saved-pass shortcut.
   Keep technical, visual, semantic, workflow and actual-author results
   separate. A complete negative observation is not an execution retry.

## Stop 4: smallest worker derivative

Derive from recovery `measurement/gate-worker.mjs`, which already separates
workspace from application and initializes a choice from its actual declared
source route. Change its explicit stage-2 assertion to stage 4, label the
source as a genuine amended-turn snapshot, and update imports/identity.
Keep its existing desktop/phone three-rest capture, entry image, real
pointer and sequential-Tab keyboard trials, opposite-state preparation,
request/error capture and all-original evidence. Do not feed the stop-2
worker a changed stage behind its guard.

Discover all actual look alternatives and aliases from stop-4 public source.
The unchanged policy counts two or three advertised looks, but discovery
must retain extra, broken and missing alternatives. A missing chooser or
unchanged/idempotent trial is not a successful choice. The existing report
schema can represent these findings. Human original review still decides
whether the looks materially differ and are usable for choosing, not merely
whether a URL, palette or hash changed. The actual recommended look is
required before the fixed turn-5 selection; no invented tie-breaker.

## Bounds, gates and focused checks

Unchanged policy: looks 900000 ms; final build and auxiliary 300000 ms each;
desktop/mobile/short 1500000 ms each; remaining four modes 900000 ms each.
Each includes the existing 5000 ms shutdown allowance. One unit at a time,
no retry or missing-mode waiver. The special twelve-batch concept replacement
is exhausted and is not silently reused as a later-stage allowance. Any
future transport subdivision would need its own prospective accounting
within the existing stage cap, not twelve new attempts or relaxed coverage.

Before the first later measurement, deterministic checks should reject:
stale recovery/result/build identities; changed original inputs, amendment,
session or saved artifacts; an incomplete or nonlatest launch turn; changed
sibling source; an escaped/symlink app root; wrong install/build cwd;
incorrect nested public-asset mapping; altered whole-wording selection;
missing/duplicate originals or units; and interrupted/repeated invocations.
Valid fixtures cover both complete positive and complete negative technical
results. No additional browser calibration, model conversation, skill prose
or corrective actor prompt is proposed here.

Turn 3 still needs its actual complete 12-to-22-beat storyboard/checklist
review before fixed turn 4. Stops 4 and 5 still require real choice handoffs.
Final technical evidence is not physical-phone or author approval. This
proposal does not lower the recorded concept visual limitations or imply
they are repaired in later output.
