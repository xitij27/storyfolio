# Candidate 2, actor-owned concept images

Root reviewed all 18 PNG originals individually with original detail, plus
the contact sheet, on 12 October 2026. This reviews retained actor images,
not a new independent browser capture or author approval. Exact file sizes
and hashes are in [the image manifest](candidate-2-stop-2-recovery/actor-checks-manifest.json).
The source archive was recovered after closure; it is not the official
turn-2 snapshot. The images preceded the final startup-wrapper change.

Coverage is complete for the retained images:

| Concept | Desktop 1440 by 960 | Phone 390 by 844 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixture | opening, middle, close | opening, middle, close |
| States | opening, middle, close | opening, middle, close |
| Lead | opening, middle, close | opening, middle, close |

All three have readable main HTML captions and distinct non-sheet
geometry. The dark, cream and gold treatment is shared and provisional,
not three later look choices. These samples show depth, coherent framing
and a visible middle-state transformation. They do not demonstrate smooth
transitions, correct settling, functional controls or fallbacks.

- Fixture: seven chunky modules with gold pins form two rows, spread apart
  in the middle, then return. Desktop labels are discernible, phone labels
  small. The prose promises a path from firmware command to observation,
  but the sampled geometry mostly spreads labelled blocks. A meaningful
  connecting relationship is not convincingly established by these frames.
- States: seven thick discs form a ring, open into a grid, then return.
  Several ring labels rotate sideways or upside down; labels become tiny
  on phone. The explanatory text honestly separates teaching states from
  observed evidence. The diagram still needs the prose to supply most of
  that distinction.
- Lead: seven short cylindrical segments form an arc, separate into two
  rows with a turned central segment, then return. It differs visibly
  from the other concepts, but its surface words are too small and blurred
  to read reliably even in desktop originals, worse on phone. The claimed
  projection-versus-observation distinction is not readable in the object.

No obvious main-caption clipping is visible at these sampled positions.
The development indicator overlaps the start of the small bottom geometry
disclaimer in phone screenshots. Concept navigation is not visible at
these captured scroll positions; that is not proof it is absent or broken.
The contact sheet is useful for coverage and large composition only, not
legibility acceptance. Root's original-image review is broader than the
actor's single contact-sheet view, corroborated by the separately saved
native image-tool events.

The displayed factual limits remain visible: personal firmware/fixture
ownership, projection versus 72-hour observed run, no confirmed radio fix,
engineering samples versus bench-tested units, and unfinished qualification.
This is not a complete site-wide factual audit. These are real sketches
with a real choice handoff, but usability and explanatory quality remain
qualified. No original frozen concept gate or full visual pass is issued.
