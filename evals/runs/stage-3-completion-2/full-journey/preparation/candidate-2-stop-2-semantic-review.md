## What the source does and does not establish

The [shared concept implementation](candidate-2-stop-2-recovery/source/files/site/content/sketches/concepts.tsx)
contains real distinctions. A's rectangular modules have pins/contacts and
spread from a two-row assembly. B's thick selector modules form a ring and
open into a grid. C's cylindrical sleeves form a curved lead and separate
into two rows, including a turned middle sleeve. All retain their pieces
through opening, middle and closing. Their common seven-piece count,
shared layout and provisional styling do not themselves fail any frozen
criterion.

The semantic weakness to inspect is specific: face labels stay the same
across all three beats, and the closing formation equals the opening.
Much of the claimed explanation is supplied by the HTML. A says the modules
open into a command-to-observation path; its source spreads labelled blocks
without an explicit connecting path. C says it separates a projection from
an observed run, but MODEL and RUN stay neighbouring labels in the same
lower grouping. These are reasons to inspect the spatial explanation and
legibility, not a new requirement for particular geometry or a source-only
visual failure. A drawing can communicate a relationship without an edge;
the actual composition must decide that question.

Root's [actor-owned image review](candidate-2-stop-2-visual-review.md) reports
readable main captions and distinct geometry, while qualifying A's path,
B's rotated/small labels and C's unreadable surface text. I read that report
as attributed evidence and did not open those images in this task. It binds
18 actor PNGs plus a contact sheet, precedes the final startup-wrapper edit
and is not the new source-bound independent production capture. The actor
opened one contact sheet, not 18 individual originals. Its broad terminal
statement that desktop/phone checks passed is not a full visual acceptance.

The public middle/closing copy preserves projection versus 72-hour observed
run, firmware/fixture authorship rather than complete sensor design, one
preliminary emissions check versus unfinished qualification, and forty
engineering samples/six bench-tested/none to customers. It explicitly
withholds a radio cause, fix or later resolution. B's state names are
teaching labels without timings; all geometry and fallback drawings are
marked illustrative, not actual hardware, traces or measured evidence.
The colleague's electronics ownership is recorded in `facts.md`, not
reassigned in the public copy. Employer/dates and unavailable photographs
or proprietary diagrams are not introduced by these concept pages.
Project page/CV/share/finish features are explicitly later scope, not falsely
claimed complete at this concept gate.

The [comparison index](candidate-2-stop-2-recovery/source/files/site/app/page.tsx)
links all three routes. Each sketch also has actual concept navigation,
Light/Dark buttons, email links, a middle fragment and a return to comparison.
Light/Dark remain provisional aliases, not a completed later look choice.
Phone navigation is absolutely positioned with wrapping and a 65% width
limit while look controls occupy the upper right. That source suggests a
possible overlap to inspect, not proof of a hit-test or focus failure.
All public routes/aliases are retained in root's exhaustive targets; no
less-preferred or possibly broken option is omitted.
