## Observations

- No main-caption clipping or horizontal overflow is visible in the entry,
  opening, middle or ending frames. Headline, personal firmware/fixture
  responsibility and illustration disclaimer are readable at both sizes.
  The middle's radio uncertainty and projection versus 72-hour distinction,
  and the ending's samples/bench/qualification limits, remain visible.
- The three-point object change is real: seven pin-bearing thick modules
  form a compact two-row assembly, spread apart in the middle, then return.
  Perspective, thickness, gold contacts and shadows give recognizable
  volume. No blank scene or sheet/card substitution is visible.
- The intended explanatory relationship is weaker than the transformation.
  In [desktop middle](../batches/01/artifacts/01-desktop/middle.png), the
  caption promises a path from command to observation, but the visual mainly
  spaces the same labels farther apart. I cannot confidently follow a
  meaningful connection or causal order from that arrangement alone. MODEL
  and RUN remain neighbouring modules; the distinction relies on prose.
  This is a material limitation, not a request for a particular geometry.
- Surface words are small and somewhat soft on desktop; on
  [phone middle](../batches/01/artifacts/01-phone/middle.png), they are too
  small for comfortable reliable reading. The HTML carries the meaning
  without requiring those face labels, but the object does not independently
  explain the work at that size. Main text and object do not overlap.
- The comparison page visibly names and explains A, B and C, recommends A
  because of personal fixture authorship, and marks styling provisional.
  All options fit in both chooser-before frames. This establishes choice
  context, not the runtime quality of B or C, which this batch does not show.
- In both viewports, pointer-before shows that comparison page and
  pointer-after shows the intended A fixture opening. Keyboard-before has
  a visible focus outline on A; keyboard-after shows the same correct
  destination. Saved reports corroborate one actual Tab press, focused and
  unobscured target geometry, activation and URL change from `/` to
  `/sketch/fixture`. No unintended alternative or stuck/idempotent response
  is visible. Duplicate after/entry bytes were still individually viewed.
- Phone entry navigation wraps C onto a second line without an observed
  collision with Light/Dark. Navigation disappearing from later resting
  frames is consistent with scroll position, not evidence it was removed.
  Other controls, alternate looks, email delivery and the return link were
  not exercised by this batch and are not inferred successful here.
