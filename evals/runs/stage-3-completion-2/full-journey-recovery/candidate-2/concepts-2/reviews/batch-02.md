## Observations

- Entry, opening, middle and ending have readable main text, attribution,
  caveats and action links in both viewports. No body-text or chooser
  clipping/overlap blocker was seen at these captured rests. The phone's
  concept navigation wraps onto a second line but remains readable.
- The same seven circular modules are visible in a ring at opening,
  rearranged into rows at the middle, and returned to a ring at ending.
  The intended distinction between a teaching model, the observed radio
  behaviour, projected endurance and the 72-hour run is stated clearly
  in the adjacent prose. The model is explicitly labelled illustrative,
  not hardware Ada built.
- Object-story clarity remains qualified: several ring labels are rotated
  or upside down; all model labels are small and soft, particularly on
  the phone. The middle arrangement is visibly different, but a causal
  power-state sequence or its link to the physical observation is not
  self-explanatory from the geometry. Much of the meaning remains in the
  prose. This is a visual/semantic limitation, not erased by the technical
  pass, and not a claim of final material-design quality.
- Both pointer-before images show the actual comparison index and the
  visible `B · Power-state selector` link. Both keyboard-before images
  show the focus outline on that same link. The four after images visibly
  show the correct concept-B heading and ring model, not just an unrelated
  URL change. Reports corroborate two Tab presses and Enter for keyboard,
  visible/in-view/enabled hit targets and `/` to `/sketch/states` response.
