## Delegated choices versus the final human gate

The [asking reference](../../../skills/storyfolio/references/asking.md)
permits the agent to select taste when the person explicitly delegates:
concept, look and order, recorded with rejected alternatives. A frozen
fictional test participant can make or delegate those choices, truthfully
labelled simulated; it cannot confirm unknown personal facts. Selection
must refer to alternatives that actually exist. The author's request to
finish authorizes necessary work, not fictional claims that they personally
scrolled or approved the result.

The Stage 3 exit is different: the original design and plan require the
real author's review of the finished site on desktop and their phone. A
coordinator's taste choice, emulated phone viewport, scripted “looks right”
or an earlier prototype approval cannot substitute. Implementation and all
machine-verifiable work should proceed now without another blanket approval
question, then report ready for that specific real-world review. Stage 4
packaging/publication and Stage 1's separately recorded closing decisions
remain distinct; no push is authorized by this completion work.
