**How the movement behaves**

The desktop journey totals about eight viewport heights, adjusted to fit real content comfortably.

Each arrangement settles long enough to read. Short transitions connect those states. Scrolling upwards reverses the movement; chapter links jump directly to the relevant content.

There is no scroll-wheel capture, mandatory dragging, continuous spinning or autoplay sound. Text remains visible throughout transitions. The 3D earns its place by showing chronology, document order, comparisons and the work index.

**The look**

The chosen palette is:

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Ground and paper | Warm ivory **#F3EEE3** |
| Main ink | Charcoal **#242824** |
| Accent | Deep brine green **#355D4A** |

Sheets have matte surfaces, fine neutral rules and restrained edge shadows. Your own photographs provide the visual specificity. The layout has space around the object, with a comfortable reading column beside it.

**Source Serif 4** handles headings and editorial text. **IBM Plex Mono** handles log fields, dates and labels. Both can be self-hosted with their licences retained.

Text stays readable: regular or medium weights, generous line spacing, roughly 60–70 characters per desktop line, and at least 17px body text on phones. Actual colour combinations must pass contrast checks. Green never carries meaning by itself.

**On a phone**

The page becomes a single-column story. Static sheet images and diagrams sit beside the content they explain.

There is no lengthy pinned camera sequence, sideways swipe requirement or tiny canvas text. The chronology expands inline. Chapter links wrap naturally, and inquiry links remain easy to reach with generous touch targets.

Subtle fades may be used when motion is allowed. Phone 3D would only be considered later if testing shows a clear benefit without harming reading or performance.

**Without the 3D**

| Situation | Visitor experience |
|---|---|
| Reduced motion | Static folio and diagrams; no camera travel, parallax or animated transitions |
| WebGL unavailable or failing | Static images replace the scene; all content and links remain |
| JavaScript disabled | Complete HTML page with native anchors, expandable details, photographs and email links |
| Keyboard or screen reader | Logical headings, visible focus, descriptive links and equivalent document content; canvas ignored by assistive technology |

No essential content depends on hover, animation or the canvas. The layout must also work at 320px width and with enlarged text.
