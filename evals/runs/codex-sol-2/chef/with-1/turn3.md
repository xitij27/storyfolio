**The central object and page structure**

Eight paper leaves form one bound log. The same leaves remain present from the first screen to the last: they open, fan out, arrange into records, display evidence and close again.

The paper format is a visual interpretation of your log, not a claim about its actual medium. Its faces carry your photographs, real records and confirmed facts. The narrative remains ordinary readable text alongside the scene.

On desktop, the log occupies a sticky area while the text scrolls beside it. The whole story takes roughly eight screen heights, adjusted to the actual content. Scrolling stays native and reversible; visitors can jump straight to a chapter or contact.

**1. Started — meet you and establish the question**

The first screen shows your name, Leeds, your Head Chef role at Saltmarsh, and the central question. The role uses **September 2022**; acting Head Chef from March can appear in the supporting detail.

The log begins closed, then opens enough to reveal your photograph of your grandmother’s ogiri pot. The image identifies a stated influence. It does not invent a childhood scene, a lesson she taught you, or a connection between that pot and a restaurant recipe.

A compact evidence preview links to the later whole-kitchen waste result. Someone who stops here still gets your identity, work, some evidence and a way to contact you.

Visitors can click:

- A chapter menu to jump anywhere.
- **Contact** to reach the three enquiry routes.
- The evidence preview to jump to **Ready**.
- The pot photograph to expand its image details.

**The look**

The visual direction comes from paper, labels and the proposed log:

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Background | Warm paper **#F3EFE5** |
| Main text | Charcoal **#242820** |
| Accent | Deep olive **#46543A** |

The leaves have matte surfaces, restrained thickness, fine rules and gentle shadows. Your real photographs supply the richer colour and texture.

**Source Serif 4** carries headings and narrative. **IBM Plex Mono** carries log fields, dates and small labels. Body text stays comfortably readable, with approximately 55–65 characters per line. Actual handwriting remains in photographs or scans, accompanied by transcripts.

The olive accent marks selected chapters, links and focus states. Meaning never depends on colour alone.

**Movement and interaction**

Each transition explains a change in the content: opening introduces you, fanning reveals fields, rows expose records, paired pages compare evidence, and closing completes the answer.

The site uses one scene rather than a new effect in every section. Settled pages remain still while visitors read. There is no required dragging, scroll trapping, autoplay sound or custom cursor.

Image details and transcripts use simple inline disclosures. If a larger overlay is later necessary, it must have a visible close button, Escape support and correct keyboard-focus behaviour.

**On a phone and without 3D**

| Situation | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Phone** | Single-column narrative with compact log illustrations between sections. No long pinned sequence. Records open at readable sizes; contact stays easy to reach. |
| **Reduced motion** | Static chapter arrangements, with no page flips, parallax or camera travel. Respect the system preference and provide a motion toggle. |
| **No WebGL** | Static images of the log arrangements beside the complete narrative, records and links. |
| **No JavaScript** | Ordinary HTML chapters, figures, transcripts, disclosures and anchor links. Contact still works. |

On a phone, navigation becomes a labelled expandable menu with a visible contact link. Nothing depends on hovering.

Keyboard navigation, screen-reader structure, visible focus, descriptive image text and text at 200% zoom are part of the planned experience. Turning motion off preserves the visitor’s reading position.
