# Smallest defensible Stage 3 integration step

12 October 2026. Read-only source/evidence audit requested by the coordinator
after the author asked to complete the skill, with Stage 3 first. This file
changes no skill, fixture, engine, measurement contract or historical result.
No model conversation or test was run for this audit. The proposals below
are not a frozen protocol, spending authorization or claim that Stage 3 is
complete.

## Decision

Use the coordinator's proposed envelope: **two fresh one-turn five-beat
builds, baseline then candidate, with at most one judge for each**. The next
bounded milestone tests the demonstrated explanatory-composition miss.
Do not add an activation turn, early sketches, optional-skill instructions,
the finishing checklist or full-site continuation to this allowance.
Preparation is not authorization to invoke the models.

The governing [stage review](../../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-11-stage-review.md)
explicitly supersedes the original plan's proposal to add stop rows before
obtaining evidence. The [post-baseline disposition](../../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-11-post-baseline-attribution.md)
also requires resolving or explicitly dispositioning the visual issue before
the early-journey question. All saved build studies use an external override
of the planning-only endpoint. None is normal build-activation evidence.

## Two exact draft visual paragraphs

Draft 1, explanatory composition, if the refreshed baseline still exhibits
the relevant miss:

> Compare the running scene with the storyboard at its settled beats. Can
> the visitor inspect the work relationship or evidence the pieces were
> meant to show, rather than only read its names? Revise the composition
> when the explanation lives only in the surrounding prose. Keep that
> accessible prose; it does not replace what the scene was meant to show.

Draft 2, readable transitions:

> Inspect the changes between beats in both scroll directions, including
> intermediate positions. When words on the pieces change, correct any
> overlap that makes the old and new words unreadable together. A readable
> settled beat does not establish a readable transition.

This is about already approved meanings. It does not require arrows,
switches, a fixed apparatus, additional controls, invented artifact detail,
the accepted prototype's geometry or a palette. HTML may explain scheduling;
accessible repetition is not failure. The fixture's evidence displays
already distinguish projection and observation, so no new generic
factuality or five-section instruction is needed.

## No supported stop-4-to-6 activation change now

Every saved build experiment explicitly overrides the shipped planning-only
endpoint. None establishes what an ordinary approved-storyboard request
does without that exception. Do not remove the endpoint or add stops 4 to
6 to attach these visual candidates.

A later bounded natural-entry observation must save whether the unchanged
skill refuses, restarts facts or continues correctly. Only a relevant
failure supports the minimum scope/routing correction, followed by that
same question again. The approved design supplies the intended gates:
spine/look choice, remaining chapters/wording choice and finishing/real
phone review. It is not evidence that extra how-to instructions are needed.
A reply-only handoff probe can inspect a declared next gate, but is not
proof of autonomous building. If the ordinary handoff succeeds, preserve
that result rather than inventing a refusal to justify new rows.

## Smallest useful next model envelope

The active proposal is **two one-turn actor conversations, at most one
judge for each**, with identical task messages and conditions in both arms.
Both receive the fixed five-beat, one-appearance contract with the same
external build permission. Finish and review the baseline before selecting
the candidate. If a quality criterion already passes, omit redundant
guidance for it. Preserve failures and stop at the declared boundary:
no automatic retry, second appearance, chapter expansion or corrective turn.

The refreshed baseline is necessary because the known ambient helper
exposure and actor-visible environment information need a fixed prospective
disposition, and the capture/source-inventory repairs change measurement
identity. Freeze any neutral helper, source visibility, isolation boundary,
engine, fixture, prompts, controls expectations and checker identically
before both arms. The previous five-beat run remains failure evidence, not
the unchanged-condition comparator for these new inputs.

This auditor initially proposed combining a short ordinary handoff turn
with each visual build. That suggestion is **not adopted**: it changes the
task kind and exceeds the one-turn allowance. Natural-entry evidence stays
a later question. The text judges cannot score visual quality; source-bound
original-image review remains separate. Actual usage must be retained;
turn deadlines are not hard token or dollar caps.

## Wording-choice candidate, separate later question

> For the opening and closing words, show three versions of each complete
> beat, including the fixed surrounding sentences. Recommend one of the
> three and leave room for the person to write their own. Put the selected
> wording on the page exactly as they chose it.

The saved two-pair copy choices and design-gap audit linked above justify
this candidate. Three means three total, not a recommendation plus three
more. It does not enter the five-beat visual candidate because this
diagnostic never asks for wording choices. Its later matched question must
preserve the old result and make the full design criterion explicit in
measurement before both arms.
