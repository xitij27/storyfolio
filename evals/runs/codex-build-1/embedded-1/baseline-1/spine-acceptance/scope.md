# Stop-4 independent diagnostic

The frozen storyboard explicitly chooses Scope, Transition and Review for
the spine. This derivative selects exactly those three beats and their
chapters. It omits the colleague and sample-count sentences assigned to
unvisited Responsibilities/Observation; other expected wording, assets,
links and static-mode policies remain unchanged. The unchanged coordinator
checker is used with separate dependencies. This is not the frozen full
seven-beat acceptance. The judge's full-site interface does not accept a
derivative expectation file, so these results remain separately reviewed
coordinator evidence and are not supplied via its --acceptance flag.
