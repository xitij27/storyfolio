# Saved screenshot review

11 October 2026. This reviewer opened all seven images listed below with
`view_image` at original resolution. Desktop captures are 1440 by 900;
phone viewport captures are 390 by 844. These observations come from the
images, not an inference from canvas counts or source code.

The desktop images support the author's concern about visual richness:
large headings sit beside lists of cream strips, with little visible
material or object detail. The phone images are legible static documents
with simple flow diagrams. They retain information but do not present a
3D object or a comparable visual story.

## Review ownership and limits

The root reviewer separately reports opening
`desktop-scope.opening.png`, `desktop-transition.diagram.png`,
`phone-state.table.png` and `reference-v3-repository-preview.jpg`.
This reviewer did not independently open those four files in this pass and
does not attribute their inspection to this review. The reference JPG is a
repository-supplied V3 preview, not a fresh rendering of the original site.

The [capture manifest](manifest.json) records Chromium software-WebGL
captures from `http://127.0.0.1:3011/`. Its phone records describe an emulated
viewport with the scene off, not a physical-phone test. These stills do not
establish transition quality, performance, keyboard operation or every
scroll position. Other looks were not visually inspected in this pass.

Visible copy is generally readable in these seven captures. That supports
readability at these positions, not a claim that the site meets the
author's aesthetic standard. The desktop limitation is principally the
simplistic object, repeated composition and lack of meaningful visual
detail, rather than missing WebGL or invisible text. The mobile result
preserves a plain document rather than that richer experience.
