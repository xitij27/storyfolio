Handover reassembles the object.
- **Opening:** Ada's firmware and fixture contribution beside the assembled object and question. **Close:** A firmware decision needs a physical observation; estimates and runs support different claims. Email and HTML CV.
- **Stands on:** User-confirmed firmware/fixture ownership, six of forty samples, lesson, preliminary qualification and battery distinction. No employer/date or fault resolution.
- **Needs from user:** Concept choice only. No missing photograph or colleague's diagram needed.
- **Why theirs:** The fixture Ada built becomes a countable argument for exactly the scope of Mosslink's evidence.

## 3. The observation path

## Recommendation and decision

Choose 1, the bring-up fixture. With captions hidden, its forty sockets and six highlights communicate the precise evidence boundary most clearly. The sensor's split banks need words to explain the evidence types; the harness's order is clear but its significance needs labels. All three clear the ownership/honesty bars; the recommendation rests on the visible counting, not those baseline requirements.

Selected: **1. The bring-up fixture**, on 2026-10-22. User explicitly instructed: “Go with the concept you recommend from the alternatives you just showed. Record which one you chose and why”.

Chosen over the sensor, whose two evidence banks need more explanation, and the probe harness, whose teaching order is visible but less specific to the available project evidence.
