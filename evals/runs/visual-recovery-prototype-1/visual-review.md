## Recovery-plan criteria

These are reasoned design observations, not binary acceptance scores.

| Criterion | Current evidence and qualification |
|---|---|
| Backdrop | The same physical parts open into command, board, fixture and observation. Connected arrows and coordinated inspection explain the relationship instead of merely restating chapter titles. HTML and authored SVG preserve that meaning without a scene. This is an illustrative relationship, not a working circuit or measured signal. |
| Depth | The opening assembly becomes an exposed relationship and returns as an evidence-led assembly. The instrument's fixed printing and repeated part identities supply continuity; the ending promotes observation versus projection rather than introducing a new object. |
| Swap | The fixture, firmware-to-bench relationship, ownership division and exact evidence bounds come from this fictional embedded case. This is substantially less interchangeable than the rejected labeled rows. Its generic instrument styling could still fit other embedded portfolios, so the work-specific explanation and claims do important work. It is not a reconstruction of a unique real apparatus. |
| Detail and hierarchy | Different case profiles, board contacts, clamps, screws, knobs and printed surfaces make parts distinguishable. Primary role words and the observation/projection display have hierarchy. Fine face printing remains supplementary and is not reliably readable at phone scale; the HTML carries all essential claims and qualifications. |
| Spatial quality | The fixture acts as a backing structure in the assembly, while the middle exposes spatial relationships. Different depths, occlusion and changing scale support that explanation. Temporary part occlusion remains visible during travel; sampled frames do not prove uninterrupted smoothness or absence of every possible intersection. The separate movement record preserves the two defects found and their corrections. |
| Phone and still modes | The narrow composition retains an active scene and usable controls. Reduced motion has a composed still scene, and no-JavaScript, no-WebGL and context-loss paths have an authored diagram. These are browser-emulated observations, not physical-phone frame-rate, battery or touch-comfort measurements. |

The prototype now supplies concrete material for the author's review
