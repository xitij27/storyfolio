## Against the author's bar

Every beat is a picture of its subject: the card catalogue, three flagged
places, the degree leaf, documents, an alert bell, two books, the
migration crossing, database cylinders, a runbook, a terminal with a
lever, a lectern, the closed book with an envelope. This is the first
Stage 3 site where that holds.

It is still short of V3. The runbook is a stage with small props set on
it; its pages never become the picture, as V3's pages become a globe or a
maze floor. Every beat uses the same book at the same angle and the camera
never moves. The places are three flags on paper, not a map or a route.
Kestrel Freight and the working documents show the same three sheets.
Lumen Pay is a blank book by design. The props are simple shapes.

The author's desktop and phone review decides.
