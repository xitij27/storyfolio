Mosslink — environmental sensor

I wrote the firmware, scheduled the power states and built the bring-up fixture. A colleague designed the printed circuit board and analogue front end. Bring-up means checking a new board’s basic operation.

Tools and working documents

C, oscilloscopes, logic analysers and board bring-up. Working documents include state tables, timing diagrams, current traces and bench checklists.

Observation and evidence

  • A current trace showed the radio remaining awake after a failed transmission. The cause, fix, timing and later outcome are not confirmed.
  • The two-year battery figure is a projection from bench current readings and an assumed reporting cycle. The longest run was 72 hours. No field endurance or weather testing supports a two-year claim.
  • Forty engineering samples were assembled; six were bench tested. None went to customers. This was not a field pilot or product launch.
  • One preliminary emissions check passed. The full qualification programme is unfinished; the device is not described as certified or qualified.

Engineering principle

A firmware decision needs a physical observation. An estimate and an observed run support different claims.
