Code meets the bench.

An estimate. An observed run.

5 / Record

Keep the scope with the result.

Forty engineering samples were assembled; six were tested on the bench. None went to customers. One preliminary emissions check passed; the full qualification programme is unfinished.

These are project counts and outcomes. They do not claim that I personally assembled or tested every sample. This was not a field pilot or a product launch.

5 / Record

The claim follows the observation.

A firmware decision needs a physical observation. An estimate and an observed run support different claims. That is the lesson I want to bring to firmware work involving physical devices.
