# Before the menu — full planning storyboard

Status: concept selected by Ines; storyboard awaiting approval. Planning only. No code, assets or site built. Chapter names and caption briefs below are proposed editorial labels, not final approved website copy.

## Direction

Question: **What happens before the menu is written?**

One continuous page follows Ines's ferment log: start date, salt, check days, readiness. Six jars with attached record labels carry the story from opening to close. They are designed information surfaces inspired by her jar wall, not six asserted real ferment batches. All personal claims refer to facts.md. The conclusion answers through her confirmed working documents and apprentice/workshop work; the menu arrives last.

The opening establishes the object's origin through an attached ferment label and its four real field names. Grandmother's pot and Katz appear as named influences in the next chapter, not as an invented origin of her logging method or recipes. No cinematic kitchen fly-through, separate props taking over, or simulated fermentation.

## Movement and interaction

- One persistent scene and six persistent jars. Smooth translations and small turns make relationships legible; no spins, forced zooms, particle effects or unrelated objects.
- Scene sits alongside or behind generous clear text space, with no moving object crossing text. Labels show short identifying facts; long content stays in HTML.
- Scroll changes arrangement rather than changing the visitor's scrolling speed. Chapter links jump to standard anchors, with the corresponding settled scene updated.
- Clicking an optional image/detail opens an accessible HTML dialog with caption/source/credit, keyboard focus management and a close button. All such content is also available through a text link; no required canvas interactions or hover-only facts.
- Motion toggle switches to fixed chapter illustrations. Tab order follows document order; visible focus and touch targets are maintained. No audio.

## A visitor with thirty seconds

The first screen exposes Ines's name, Head Chef at Saltmarsh in Leeds, fermentation/apprentice focus, one scoped team result and a visible contact shortcut. The opening question is the selected concept question, pending final copy approval. Six chapter links permit direct jumps; Contact skips to the ending. Nobody has to finish the animation to understand the work or reach Ines.

## Look and type

Three material treatments are planned for later visual comparison. They do not change the content or structure. No separate appearance-picker is needed in the public visitor flow.

| Treatment | Ground | Ink | Accent | Jar/label surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Larder daylight — recommended starting point | Warm off-white #F4F0E7 | Charcoal #242722 | Deep moss #465C40 | Clear simplified glass, restrained amber tint, matte cream labels |
| Night shelf | Near-black olive #171E19 | Warm white #F4F0E7 | Light ochre #D8B568 | Dark bottle edges, pale labels, soft directional light |
| Label paper | Pale grey-green #E9EDE5 | Dark green #24382B | Dark rust #98432D | Almost colourless glass, subtly textured paper, minimal shadows |

Use surface colour for atmosphere rather than colour alone for meaning. Verify text/control contrast in any later build. Avoid expensive multi-layer refraction; readable silhouettes and labels matter more than glass realism.

Type proposal: **Source Sans 3** for body, headings and navigation; **IBM Plex Mono** for record fields, dates and jar labels. Self-host licensed font files in a future build, with system fallbacks. No handwriting imitation presented as Ines's handwriting. Labels remain readable at the final settled size.
