**Before the menu** will be one continuous story carried by a 3D ferment register. Its question is: **What happens before the menu is written?**

Seven leaves stay present throughout the scroll. They open, separate into informative arrangements and finally bind together again. The movement explains your work: dates become a sequence, log fields become a process, and results become readable evidence.

The complete plan is saved in [storyboard.md](/tmp/storyfolio-evals/codex-chef-terse-with-XpX4fT/docs/story/storyboard.md). Nothing has been built.

### The whole scroll

| Chapter | What visitors learn | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry** | Your name, Head Chef at Saltmarsh, fermentation focus and the central question | The register opens, revealing the log fields you use |

The desktop scroll would take roughly **ten screen lengths**. Each movement ends in a settled, readable state. On phones, content determines the length.

The opening immediately gives visitors your name, role, evidence of your method and enquiry links. A chapter rail lets them jump anywhere, including straight to contact.

### Visual direction

I recommend **warm paper, charcoal ink and dark olive accents**: a working register with subtle paper texture, fine rules and generous margins.

Use **Source Sans 3** for readable text and **IBM Plex Mono** for dates, quantities and log fields. Two alternative looks are documented: mineral grey with blue accents, or cream with rust accents.

The register is an editorial interpretation of your log. It won’t pretend to reproduce real batch entries, recipes or handwriting.

### Motion and accessibility

Scroll controls the same seven leaves throughout. The camera stays steady while visitors read; there is no forced scrolling or continuous spinning.

All information also exists as ordinary page text:

- **Phone:** a single column with a smaller scene.
- **Reduced motion:** static arrangements without rotation or parallax.
- **No WebGL:** a static register illustration and readable chapter panels.
- **No JavaScript:** the complete page, navigation and contact links still work.
