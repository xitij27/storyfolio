# Portfolio plan for Marta Kowalczyk

The site follows a single idea: **good architecture connects an understanding of a place with the care of making it**.

Visitors travel through the order of a drawing set—location, plans, sections, elevations, details, schedules—while seeing your work, your responsibilities, and your approach. Your cabin provides the continuous 3D thread. Calder Court demonstrates technical design and delivery at a larger scale. Teaching has a clear place and contact route.

The drawing-set sequence shapes the experience, but the public headings use ordinary language. Someone with an old house should understand the page without knowing architectural terminology.

This is a complete planning specification. Nothing is being built.

## 1. Site structure

The site needs:

- **Homepage:** the connected story described below.
- **Four project pages:** Cabin at Kashuby, Calder Court, Kirk Street, and A School That Grows.
- **Teaching page:** your approach, experience, and guest-critic invitation.
- **Contact section:** on the homepage, directly linkable from every page.
- **Accessible CV:** downloadable and accompanied by a readable HTML biography.

The homepage should take roughly three to five minutes to explore attentively, while allowing visitors to reach a project or contact information immediately.

## 3. Look and feel

The visual language should resemble a carefully composed architectural publication, with generous images and legible drawings.

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Background | Warm paper `#F3F0E8` |
| Main text and lines | Graphite `#242826` |
| Secondary surfaces | Stone `#D8D5CC` |
| Links and active states | Deep green `#315B4C` |
| Occasional material accent | Timber brown `#946747` |
| Major headings | Newsreader |
| Body, navigation, drawing labels | IBM Plex Sans |

Self-host the fonts. Keep body text around 18px on desktop and 16–18px on phones. Technical labels must remain readable, including inside enlarged drawings.

Use chapter numbers and small drawing-set labels consistently. Let photographs provide texture; avoid decorative drafting marks, artificial paper textures, and ornamental interface elements.

Motion should be slow enough to follow and short enough to preserve reading momentum. No scroll hijacking, custom cursor, autoplay soundtrack, or introductory loading gate.

## 4. Supporting project pages

Every project page needs a direct URL and this basic structure:

1. Strong opening image or drawing
2. Project facts, authorship, and your role
3. The challenge
4. Selected decisions and supporting evidence
5. Drawings and photographs
6. Outcome, with qualifications where relevant
7. Credits
8. Related project and contact links

Specific emphasis:

- **Cabin:** setting, spatial decisions, hand drawings, construction sequence, joint, time-lapse, and personal account.
- **Calder Court:** partner-led concept, your technical-to-handover responsibility, permitted wall detail, site delivery, and explicitly modelled performance.
- **Kirk Street:** conversion strategy, permitted section and photographs, and your confirmed contribution.
- **School:** competition status, four-person authorship, your structure and sections, and readable selected boards.

The homepage 3D sequence does not need to repeat automatically on project pages. Static illustrations provide the evidence; an optional model viewer can be considered later.

## 5. Phone and fallback requirements

The phone layout is designed separately around a single reading column. It does not depend on pinned scrolling.

The complete site must work with:

- JavaScript unavailable
- WebGL unavailable or failing
- Reduced motion enabled
- Keyboard navigation
- Slow connections
- Images or video still loading

Static images replace every meaningful model state. All explanatory text remains ordinary page content. Visitors can enlarge drawings, browse galleries, navigate projects, and contact you without 3D.

On capable devices, 3D loads progressively after the opening content. A failed model should leave the static illustration in place. Video loads on request.
