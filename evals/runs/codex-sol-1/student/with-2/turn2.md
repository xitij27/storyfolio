**Colours and type**

I chose warm paper and dark ink:

- Background: **#F3EFE6**
- Cards: **#FFFCF4**
- Text: **#242824**
- Accent: deep green **#285745**

Fine printed rules, subtle paper edges and restrained shadows make it feel like a working record. Green won’t mean “passed”; every status is written explicitly.

**Source Sans 3** handles reading text and headings. **IBM Plex Mono** handles card fields and small record labels. Both remain legible at ordinary sizes.

The documented alternatives are a cool white/technical-blue drawing-sheet look and a dark workbench/brass look. Warm paper is my recommendation.
