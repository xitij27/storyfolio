**The look**

I’ve chosen **warm workshop paper**.

| Element | Colour |
|---|---|
| Page ground | Warm off-white `#F3EFE6` |
| Card surface | Light paper `#FFFCF4` |
| Text | Charcoal `#242824` |
| Accent | Deep green `#285745` |

The surfaces have fine printed rules, restrained edge shadows and subtle thickness. Photographs and CAD remain the visual focus. Status is always written in words; green does not automatically mean “passed.”

**Source Sans 3** handles reading text and headings. **IBM Plex Mono** handles card fields and small record labels.

Desktop body text is around 18px, with comfortable line lengths. The name has presence without occupying an empty screen. The paper has no fake handwriting, grease stains, signatures or invented measurements.

This material treatment is a design choice, not a claim about your workshop’s appearance.
