**The look**

The selected palette comes from paper records and labels:

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|

| Main text | Dark ink **#242923** |
| Accent | Brine green **#465B3B** |

The leaves have restrained grain, subtle edges and shallow shadows. Photographs provide most of the visual richness. Their documentary character should remain intact.

**Source Serif 4** handles reading text and large editorial headings. **IBM Plex Mono** handles dates, field names and records. Both would be self-hosted, with dependable fallback fonts.

Body text stays comfortably sized—approximately 18px on desktop and 17px on phones—with generous line spacing. Essential information is never reduced to tiny labels to accommodate the scene.

**Movement and navigation**

The desktop sequence initially targets roughly **eight viewport lengths**, with settled reading states between transitions.

A desktop chapter rail links to the five chapters. On phones, a compact chapter menu provides the same anchors. A contact shortcut is available throughout.
