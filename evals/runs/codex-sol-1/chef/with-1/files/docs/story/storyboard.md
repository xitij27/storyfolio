## Story premise

**Question:** What happens before the menu is written?

**Answer at the close:** Preparation, keeping records, checking readiness and sharing the work. This is a content intention, not finished personal copy. It rests on F08/F10/F12 and the confirmed team result F02.

One object carries the whole page: eight paper leaves from an editorial interpretation of Ines's ferment log. They stay visible throughout the desktop scene and return to an open log at the close. Their faces carry owned photographs, confirmed facts, field names and work entries. A record can become readable, a photograph can become an exhibit, and several entries can become an index; the shapes remain the same. Removing the scene removes these spatial relationships, so the fallback restores them as ordinary figures and grouped records.

The design does not claim that the real log is eight pages, that it is loose-leaf, or that its entries include the other work shown here. This is the website's organising form.

## Phone and fallback behaviour

All names, claims, links and navigation are ordinary semantic HTML. WebGL enhances the same content; text is never available only as a texture. No hover-only controls.

| Where | Experience |
|---|---|
| Phone | One-column reading; static log/photo composition by default, eight leaves represented within normal figures. A brief lightweight transition may be enabled on capable devices after review, but no fixed full-height canvas, long pinned stretch or horizontal scrolling. Contact links have at least 44px targets. Navigation does not cover text |
| Reduced motion | Static composition per chapter; no sweeping camera, unfolding, parallax or animated scroll. Anchor links jump immediately. Full information and actions preserved |
| No WebGL / lost context / slow device | Exported still compositions of the same eight leaves, plus HTML records/photos. Canvas failure replaces the scene without hiding content |
| No JavaScript | Full ordered HTML page, images, section anchors and ordinary contact links. Work details appear inline or in native disclosure elements. No loading gate |

## Look and type

Selected look uses the paper material of the object. Alternatives are documented for later visual comparison; this planning stage does not implement a palette switcher.

| Look | Ground | Ink | Accent | Leaf surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kitchen log — selected | Warm paper #F3EFE4 | Near-black #242923 | Brine green #465B3B | Off-white #FFFCF4, restrained grain, light edge wear, shallow shadows |
| Label drawer — alternate | Chalk #F7F5EE | Graphite #292B2D | Rust #94452C | Matte cream labels, clean ruled lines |
| Evening larder — alternate | Charcoal #202722 | Cream #F5F0E6 | Pale olive #C1CD98 | Warm pale leaves against a dark ground |

Reading type: **Source Serif 4**, regular and semibold. Log field labels/date records: **IBM Plex Mono**, regular and medium. Self-host licensed font files in a future build; use robust local fallbacks. Body text at least 18px on desktop and 17px on phone, comfortable line height and roughly 60-character lines. Large editorial headings, no cursive chef signature or illegible texture. Evaluate contrast on all actual rendered combinations; never make colour the only status signal.

Motion: slow, purposeful rearrangements with no infinite orbit or idle wobble. Camera framing keeps the active record readable; a leaf turns only to expose its content. Do not lock headings into tiny labels to accommodate 3D. Owned photographs should preserve their documentary character.
