# Decisions delegated by Ines

Authority: user said “For anything I have not covered, use your judgement and tell me what you assumed. Carry on.” Nothing has been built.

| Decision | Why | How to change it |
|---|---|---|
| Choose The Ferment Log over The Prep List and The Larder | Actual working document; makes checking and readiness carry the story; explains why the menu comes last | Choose another concept in concepts.md |
| One continuous page, eight paper leaves throughout | A log provides a recognisable physical object; each leaf carries evidence rather than background effects | Change object or chapter count in storyboard.md |
| Treat loose leaves and chapter grouping as an editorial interpretation | We do not know the physical format of Ines's log | Supply its actual format; adjust the object |
| Opening rooted in grandmother's pot photograph | Own asset and stated influence; does not require third-party art | Select a phone photograph instead |
| Primary look: warm log paper, dark ink, brine green; two alternate treatments retained | Comes from paper labels, records and larder imagery | Pick either alternate palette |
| Source Serif 4 for reading, IBM Plex Mono for log fields | Readable narrative paired with clear dates/records; self-host fonts in a future build | Substitute licensed fonts with similar roles |
| Eight desktop viewport lengths maximum as an initial target; shorter natural flow on phones | Enough movement for the story without exhausting task-focused visitors | Adjust after usability review |
| Contact routes for apprentices, writers and workshops; no recruiting-oriented CV | Audience explicitly given; user is not job hunting | Add a CV or other audience later |
| Use owned photos first; restaurant photos optional and Brine illustrations pending | Possession does not establish all reproduction permissions | Record permission and asset credit before including |
| Do not publish unconfirmed numbers or invent log records | Figures and recipe details need evidence | Confirm specifics in facts.md |
| No analytics, chat, autoplay sound or newsletter at launch | No demonstrated need; simpler site and maintenance | Add a tool only for a defined purpose |
| English initially; static content first; no booking system assumed | Material is English; booking and contact arrangements unknown | Supply languages and booking/contact destinations |
| Generic contact entry points proposed, no invented addresses or vacancies | Routes matter, operational details are absent | Supply actual public addresses/forms/booking links |

These are design decisions. No assumption is used to turn an unresolved personal fact into a claim.
