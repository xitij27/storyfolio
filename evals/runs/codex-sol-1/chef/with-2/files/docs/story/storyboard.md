## Idea

Question: **What happens before the menu is written?**

The answer is staged in the order Ines supplied: the prep list and ferment log precede the menu. One small shelf of six labelled jars carries the visitor through starting, salting, checking, readiness and use. The labels—not glass effects—carry the information. A menu sheet enters only near the end. The six jars are a designed set of chapter carriers, not six claimed recipes, a live inventory or one batch's documented history.

The opening includes Ines's owned photograph of her grandmother's ogiri pot and source-based influences. It does not morph the pot into a jar, suggest the jar wall began with it, or invent a family recipe. This is her stated influence, not a fabricated origin story.

Deep links show the relevant scene without playing previous chapters. Reverse scrolling reverses arrangement only; it does not pretend to reverse fermentation. No cursor tricks, auto-play sound or drag gate.

## Look and type

Choose **Paper and olive** for the first direction. Other looks are alternatives for later review, not a plan to build three themes.

| Look | Ground | Ink | Accent | Piece surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper and olive — selected | Warm paper #F2EBDD | Near-black #252820 | Deep olive #52613D | Clear, lightly tinted glass; bone-white labels; restrained dark lids |
| Larder at dusk | Charcoal #252820 | Warm paper #F2EBDD | Light ochre #D8B66C | Smoky glass, off-white labels, soft directional light |
| Workbench | Chalk #F7F4EC | Blue-black #26353A | Deep brick #8A4334 | Clear glass, chalk labels, muted metal lids |

Primary text: **Source Sans 3**, with large clear headings, generous leading and a readable 60–70-character measure. Labels and log fields: **IBM Plex Mono**, for the document's date/salt/check structure. Both self-hosted, subset and limited weights. No tiny handwriting font, fabricated manuscript marks or distressed texture behind text. Actual photographed handwriting can be shown alongside legible transcription.

Palette comes from paper labels and the practical larder surface, not neon liquid or stock luxury restaurant styling. Olive is an interface accent, not an invented signature ingredient. Use modest reflected light and shadows for dimensionality. Glass must not hide its label. Validate normal text at WCAG AA contrast before implementation; never depend on colour to identify field states.

## Practical build brief for later

- One lightweight scene using six reused jar meshes and flat readable label surfaces. Prefer restrained materials to costly layered refraction. No physics engine or video background required.
- HTML, CSS, essential images and contact content render before any 3D module. Lazy-load desktop enhancement; phones receive the static experience by default. A static substitute exists for every chapter state.
- Responsive image crops must be checked for supplier prices and uncleared faces. Approved display label excerpts must omit prices and unnecessary identifying details; retain originals privately.
- Performance targets, not promises: LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms, CLS ≤0.1 at the 75th percentile once live. Before launch test a mid-range phone and throttled connection, tune scene fidelity to preserve responsiveness, and reserve image/canvas dimensions. Assess deployed data after launch rather than claiming an unmeasured score.
- Verify keyboard use, screen-reader reading order, reduced motion, WebGL failure, JavaScript disabled, deep links, reverse scroll and 200% zoom. Labels in the canvas are never the only content source. Menus/modals must preserve focus and have explicit close controls.
- Review captions against the fact sheet, cleared image/quote credits, contact destinations, current workshop information, and factual scope of waste result before publication.
