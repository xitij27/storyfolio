## Colour and typography

The palette should feel like paper, ink, and the working environment:

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Main background | Warm ivory — `#F4F1E9` |
| Main text | Deep ink — `#182B30` |
| Links and key accents | Dark teal — `#17635E` |
| Secondary surfaces | Pale grey-green — `#DEE7E2` |
| Sparse annotations | Amber — `#C58B36` |

Amber is for marks and highlights, with dark text where needed. All text and interactive states need contrast checks.

Use **Source Serif 4** for large statements and reflective passages, paired with **Source Sans 3** for body copy, navigation, and evidence. The serif gives your voice presence; the sans keeps the detailed work easy to read.

Large headlines, generous margins, restrained annotation, and authentic artifacts will do more for the identity than decorative interface elements.

## How the motion earns its place

Use one coherent visual language: paper layers, spatial relationships, alignment.

- **3D explains structure:** layers reveal how information is organised.
- **Scroll explains change:** an observed problem connects to its intervention.
- **Small interactions support reading:** project links respond subtly to pointer or keyboard focus.
- **Evidence stays available:** numbers and explanations remain ordinary selectable text.

Limit the homepage to two substantial animated sequences: the opening sheet and the scanning story. Let the remaining sections breathe.

## On a phone

The mobile site should be composed specifically for a narrow screen.

- Use a static or lightly animated rendering of the opening object.
- Replace extended pinned scenes with short, vertically stacked steps.
- Put before-and-after sheets in sequence, with readable detail views.
- Keep navigation and contact controls easy to reach and tap.
- Make all explanations visible without hover.
- Support portrait and landscape without horizontal scrolling.

For reduced-motion settings, show the final compositions immediately and retain the full story. Keyboard and screen-reader visitors get the same information through semantic headings, text, and links.
