3. **Medication scanning: the flagship case study**

   Lead with:

   **“Understanding the workarounds behind missed scans.”**

   Explain the 40 hours of observation, interviews with 22 nurses and the three workarounds. A scrolling workflow diagram shows the practical obstacles and corresponding changes.

   Make attribution explicit:

   **Your contribution:** conducting the observation, identifying the three workarounds, and getting scanners moved to the medication room door.

   **The ward’s work:** pharmacy, IT, night staff and you collaborated on the improvements.

   **The reported result:** ward scanning rose from 71% to 94% over nine months.

   Visually separate contribution from outcome so a visitor cannot mistake the result for a solo achievement. The poster credit and study scope sit beside the evidence.

   The full case study should explain what you found, what you influenced, how colleagues contributed and what the outcome establishes.
