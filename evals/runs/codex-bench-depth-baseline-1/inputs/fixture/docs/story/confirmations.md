fictional input for `bench-depth-1`, not a quotation from any real person
or from an earlier planning conversation. It authorizes a meaning-level
story for this diagnostic only. Model execution still requires the
coordinator's separate run decision.

- The approved story has five beats, in order: `question`, `scheduling`,

  headings, explanations and connecting prose. No exact approved sentences

  not a sheet or card metaphor. Choose its piece count, form, materials,

  explanatory model, not a depiction of an actual Mosslink device.

  illustrative. It does not supply actual firmware, timings or current

  observed bench duration distinct.

- Keep the story documents current with decisions, asset changes,

Approval of this storyboard is not a claim that a site already exists,
that it has been reviewed by the real repository author, or that a physical
phone has been tested. A new output needs its own review.
