# Prototype handover

## Starting state

This is a fictional, approved five-beat story awaiting implementation. The
original fixture folder contains five story documents and two explanatory
SVGs, not a user application or an existing portfolio. The coordinator may
then copy the unchanged engine and prepare locked dependencies. Read its
setup evidence to distinguish supplied files from that preparation.

## Approved scope

Use `facts.md`, `confirmations.md`, `storyboard.md` and `assets.md`. Implement
one local appearance with beat IDs `question`, `scheduling`, `bench`,
`evidence`, `scope`. Decide the composition of an illustrative physical
bench apparatus. Do not infer exact geometry, a fixed piece count or visual
design from earlier work. All five meanings and evidence limits remain
accessible in still/failure modes. Normal desktop, phone and short-height
modes retain the 3D story.

This is a diagnostic prototype, not a completed six-stop journey, choice
of looks, wording approval, full-site checklist or permission to publish.
No actor implementation, tests, browser inspection or author review has
happened yet. Setup checks are not the actor's own preflight.

## Update before stopping

Record what you changed, design decisions, provenance, commands and actual
results. Say whether all five beats are reviewable and describe any failed
or incomplete check. Distinguish source implementation, successful build,
browser observation and real-person approval. Record remaining work without
starting a new turn or claiming a physical-phone review from a viewport
emulation. Keep everything needed to continue in this project.
