## Tradeoff and limits

The denser middle, evidence and closing captions leave a smaller scene on this
viewport. The closing assembly is approximately 160 pixels wide. This is a
deliberate tradeoff in the response to narrative depth, not a solution to
physical object scale. The fine face print is not dependable reading material
on a phone; the HTML carries all essential claims and explanations.

No clipped current-beat copy or control collision was found in these ten
originals. The source footer is outside their sampled beat rests and is not
covered by this visual claim. These software-rendered Chromium captures do
not establish physical-phone performance, comfort during actual scrolling or
the author's acceptance of the revised depth.
