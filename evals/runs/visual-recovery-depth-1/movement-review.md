## Result

No new desktop blocker was found in the inspected movement, connector or
selection evidence. The five-beat revision adds a useful explanatory order:
the question, scheduling, the bench relationship, evidence provenance, and
sample scope. This is more than adding two headings to the old page.

The additional depth remains an authored explanation of limited fictional
source material. Whether it meets the author's requested story depth still
needs their verdict. The review does not convert functional checks, extra
sections or distinctive object poses into visual approval.

### Settled story and meaningful poses

- `opening.assembly.png` retains the grouped bench assembly, plain-language
  firmware explanation, and the question connecting a decision to an
  observation. `initial.png` also preserves the initial navigation context.
- `power.cycle.png` promotes the command unit and board together above a
  broad fixture, while the observation unit becomes smaller. The HTML
  Sleep, Measure, Transmit and return sequence is readable and explicitly
  labelled a teaching loop, not Mosslink's actual firmware. This explains
  scheduling; the 3D arrangement focuses the relevant parts. It does not
  animate a real power-state sequence or reconstruct firmware.
- `middle.relationship.png` separates four parts into a readable circuit
  of explanatory arrows: command to board, board to fixture, fixture to
  observation. Its copy explains bring-up and the fixture's role rather
  than leaving the visitor with four unexplained nouns.
- `proof.boundaries.png` distinctly enlarges the observation unit. HTML
  separates measured sleep and transmit current plus an assumed reporting
  interval, supporting the two-year projection, from the separate 72-hour
  longest observed run. The provenance distinction is materially clearer
  than displaying the two numbers alone. No measured trace or exact
  current value is invented in this exhibit.
- `close.evidence.png` regroups the assembly and reduces the observation
  unit from its evidence emphasis. The ending adds 40 assembled, six
  bench-tested and zero customer samples, with six highlighted cells in a
  forty-cell count. Its visible caveat says the count is not a pass/fail
  chart, and the unfinished qualification statement remains readable.
  The ending's strongest new distinction is this HTML scope explanation,
  not a radically new object effect.

Text stays in the left reading column and the scene stays to the right in
the settled views. Principal face labels remain recognizable, but fine
face print is softer than HTML and is not the sole carrier of facts. This
is a detailed illustrative object, not a photograph of actual equipment.

govern the permitted narrative. They explicitly omit the lab-notes incident
of a failed transmission leaving the radio awake because the source does
not establish who diagnosed or fixed it, a measured outcome, or a public
incident account. Its absence here is a deliberate scope boundary, not a
forgotten ready-to-publish debugging story. The new scheduling explanation
must not be described as a replacement account of that incident.

This record reviews desktop originals only. Other viewport/fallback
images and reports exist in the full capture and need their separate
inspection. The complete run has 76 images across seven conditions and
unchanged source; only the two stale Board-description assertions fail.
That factual result is not seven visually approved conditions.

Movement evidence is a 26-image sample of continuous scrolling, not a
video, an exhaustive every-frame review, a frame-rate measurement, a
physical-phone test or a hardware GPU result. Screenshot requests span
time while scrolling continues, so the report preserves both starting
and ending positions rather than claiming exact frozen target poses.
No new paid agent/model evaluation was run by the capture harness. Author
review of story depth and a real-phone check remain outside this record.
