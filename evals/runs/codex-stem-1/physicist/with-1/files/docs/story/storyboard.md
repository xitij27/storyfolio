Use **Source Sans 3** for headings, paragraphs and links, and **IBM Plex Mono** for sheet labels, index numbers and technical notation. Self-host licensed subsets. Body roughly 18px desktop / 17px mobile; measure around 60–70 characters. Large headings use restrained weight and spacing. Long evidence text stays out of rotating textures. Color never solely communicates publication status; all final combinations need contrast checking.

Motion: scroll determines sheet spacing/orientation, with shallow depth and slow camera shifts. No autonomous endless motion, detector fly-through, particle field or invented signal trace. A transformation introduces a relationship, settles for reading, then changes when the next chapter is reached. Object occupies a side of the desktop viewport, with text beside it rather than occluded by it.
