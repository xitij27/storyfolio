Selected work — Kestrel Freight

Data-centre exit · August 2020–October 2022

I wrote and sequenced the migration plan: 310 services, two data centres, 14 dependency-based waves over 26 months, with about 60 engineers across teams. Both data centres closed on schedule.

Measured Order API availability over those 26 months: 99.98%. There were two internal incidents, each under ten minutes.

Approach: dependency mapping, rehearsed rollback, mirrored read traffic, dual writes for stateful services, weighted routing, SLO checks and a two-week soak before decommissioning.

Orders database · 2022

I led the 4 TB Postgres 11 → 15 upgrade with one database engineer. Logical replication, dual writes through ferry and hourly checksum comparisons for three weeks preceded moving reads, then writes. No maintenance window; the old cluster stayed warm for a month as the way back.

Migration runbook · 2021–now

I wrote the first version and still own it; 40 teams use it. Six stages, each with an exit check: preflight, shadow, dual write, cutover, soak, decommission.

On-call · 2021–2022

We reviewed the alerts as a team of nine. We deleted alerts nobody acted on and introduced SLO burn-rate alerts on the services that mattered. Pages fell from 85 to 22 per week over about a year.

Open source and speaking
