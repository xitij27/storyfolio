# Ordinary-entry source observation

Coordinator observation of the closed single baseline, not a paid judge or
browser verdict. Read only the retained conversation, result/process record,
public story records and relevant application source. No model, build,
browser, test or actor-source change was made by this review.

## Findings

| Question | Source-bound observation |
|---|---|
| Ordinary entry | Demonstrated. The actor accepted the ordinary build request and replaced the starter story with an authored apparatus and all five approved meaning records. It did not refuse at the planning endpoint or restart the concept/storyboard journey. Runnable quality remains a separate browser question. |
| Scope and decision sequence | Observed gate miss. Before implementation, the actor asked a text-only Warm/Dark/delegate question, then explicitly proceeded with Warm without an answer. It implemented all five chapters before an actual look decision. This is a sequencing observation, not a claim that five chapters are inherently wrong or that a provisional default equals fabricated approval. |
| Reviewable alternatives | Two source-defined alternatives, Warm and Night, plus ordinary appearance buttons exist. Source names, color tokens and buttons do not prove two materially reviewable looks or working switches. Those properties await the separate frozen browser observation. |
| Terminal choice delivery | Not demonstrated in the retained run. There is no completed final response presenting runnable alternatives and asking the author to choose. The early text-only question is not that runnable-look handoff. Because the turn timed out while checking, whether a later final handoff would have happened is unobserved. |
| Honest handover | Positive but incomplete evidence. The saved records explicitly say no answer arrived, Warm is provisional and not personal approval, and final review/commit remain unfinished. The actor corrected its own acceptance account: overlapping rebuilds invalidated earlier browser runs, and an exit-143 partial run was not a pass. This review does not independently certify its other technical or visual claims. |

The practical distinction is important: ordinary entry worked, but the
intended build-a-reviewable-spine, obtain-a-look-decision, then-expand sequence
was not followed in the observed work. The actor's honest admission of a
provisional choice does not supply the missing decision. Neither that miss
nor the deadline establishes that the two rendered alternatives are poor.
No new skill instruction, correction or rerun is authorized by this note.
