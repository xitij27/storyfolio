## A relationship carried by the object

The old
`../codex-stem-1/physicist/with-1/files/docs/story/storyboard.md` mostly
paired documents with their roles and public records with attribution.
It correctly left the actual procedure and a quantitative display pending,
because neither was supplied. Its reference comparison did not say what
the three timing paths were or what calibration choice Iris made.

The new plans have three concrete visual relationships:

1. A common reference is compared with three separately labelled paths.
   Signed positions distinguish A and C reporting late from B reporting
   early. Their unequal offsets explain why one common shift would leave
   differences between paths unchanged.
2. Corrections calculated from the first pulse group stay fixed while the
   path records move into the held-back checking group. In `with-2`, the
   correction sheet explicitly remains stationary. The movement explains
   that the check data did not set the corrections.
3. Before and after path means use a signed scale with a shared zero. B
   stays below zero and all three corrected endpoints remain nonzero.
   The summary then takes the absolute values of the three means, with
   equal weight per path, rather than presenting a generic improvement bar.

These are specified comparisons and procedural dependencies, not just
headings displayed on paper. The page can still explain them with static
diagrams, an ordinary table and accessible HTML. That equivalent content
does not make the planned spatial arrangement decorative.

The backend contrast in
`../codex-stem-1/backend/with-1/files/docs/story/storyboard.md` works for
the same reason: a later illustrative request has the same key as an earlier
request, so their arrangement explains repeat recognition. Its benchmark is
kept separate. The new physics example likewise specifies what is compared
and keeps reference-check evidence distinct from publications and wider
scientific outcomes. Unlike the backend's explicitly invented explanatory
requests, the physics numbers are supplied truth within this fictional
fixture, not values invented by either test agent.

## Scientific and ownership boundaries checked

Both final plans preserve the full table:

| Path | Frozen first-group correction | Held-back mean before | Held-back mean after |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | +1.8 ns | +2.0 ns | +0.2 ns |
| B | -0.9 ns | -1.0 ns | -0.1 ns |
| C | +2.7 ns | +3.0 ns | +0.3 ns |

The operation is raw residual minus saved correction. Both explain that
subtracting B's negative correction moves its reported time later. Both
identify a residual as reported time minus the pulse's reference time, and
define positive as late and negative as early.

The two check columns describe the same second pulse group. The first group
sets the corrections before the second is examined. `with-1` shows the
held-back before values in its earlier explanatory Compare chapter, but
labels them as such and subsequently separates them from the first-group
correction values. It does not claim the check group fitted those values.
`with-2` calls its chapter Independent check, with the procedure making
clear that this means held-back pulses, not independent external replication.

The reported measure is the mean absolute value of the three path means,
equally weighted: 2.0 ns before, 0.2 ns after, ratio ten. Neither plan treats
it as individual-pulse spread, an uncertainty estimate or scientific
sensitivity. Both retain rounded values, nonzero residuals, unknown offset
causes, no result on scientific data, and no established validity after
conditions change. Neither supplies invented raw records, error bars,
hardware geometry or cable lengths. `with-2` also specifies that animation
between endpoints is a visual transition, not a measured time series, and
does not count through invented intermediate values.

Both credit Iris's chosen procedure, implementation and check, and the lab
engineer's reference distribution hardware. They reproduce the permitted
extract credit. The 2024 consortium paper, separate 2026 preprint under
review and supervisor-led grant retain their status and ownership limits.
The example is not presented as evidence that Iris designed the whole
detector, led the science analysis or personally won the grant.

Both endings specify the answer: fix per-path corrections from one group,
check them on another, then state the measured result with its limits.
They do so while leaving polished captions for later. The later
closing-answer skill candidate was not present in these conversations.

## Diversity remains a separate question

Both agents propose the same three document families: calibration protocol,
laboratory notebook and uncertainty budget or ledger. Sharing paper is not
itself a failure; the comparison concerns their story structures.

In `with-1`, the protocol and notebook both move from reference and signed
offsets through a per-path decision to a held-back check, limits and public
record. The budget uses almost the same progression, with unknowns made
more prominent. Its judge marks diversity false and calls the alternatives
near-equivalent document arrangements. The saved concepts support that
reading.

In `with-2`, the protocol has eight chapters, and the notebook and ledger
have seven. The notebook again follows decision, procedure, check and
limits; the ledger changes the question to what the result permits one to
claim and pairs supported claims with unstated ones. Its judge marks
diversity true, but the underlying reference/correction/check/limits sequence
is still shared. A different chapter count or document name alone would
not establish a distinct direction.

The formal outcome is one diversity pass and one failure. Manual review
finds a difference in emphasis for the ledger, but does not establish that
these are three substantially different stories. Preserve the mixed scores
and this limitation; do not turn the second pass into a broad claim about
creative range. These are different plans and prompts, not repeated
judgements of identical evidence.

The second judge's summary also notes that final captions remain unwritten.
That is a planning-stage limit already required by the skill, not an
additional failed criterion or a reason to draft unsupported prose. Actual
readability, pacing and explanatory success still require a built-site test
and the author's concept review.
