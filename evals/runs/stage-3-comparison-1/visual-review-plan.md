# Pre-outcome visual review plan

12 October 2026. Prepared before this reviewer has inspected output from
either new arm. Coordinator-only review instructions, not actor input or
skill guidance. No actor/judge call, skill edit or acceptance verdict is
made here.

The approved envelope is one fresh baseline and, only if a supported miss
remains, one fresh candidate: one actor turn each, at most one text judge
each, 15-minute actor and 10-minute judge deadlines, no retries. This plan
adds no invocation or continuation. The frozen input manifest, not this
reviewer's copy of a live file, determines each arm's identity.

## Fixed question and criteria

Apply the existing visual criteria in
[the original diagnostic protocol](../../builds/bench-depth-1/protocol.md#visual-questions-independent-of-the-accepted-design)
and [the new comparison protocol](../../builds/bench-depth-2/protocol.md).
Do not change the original
[text rubric](../../builds/bench-depth-1/criteria.md), its eligibility or
its scores. The [evidence audit](../stage-3-integration-1/evidence-audit.md)
identifies two candidate-relevant observations within those visual criteria:

1. At settled beats, does the scene make the approved relationship or
   evidence inspectable, rather than merely display disconnected names
   while surrounding prose supplies the explanation? The bench relationship
   is command, board response, fixture access and observation. The evidence
   beat distinguishes a supported projection from the separate observed
   run. Assess what is actually visible, not merely declared in source.
2. Across sampled forward and reverse travel, do changing face words remain
   readable without old/new text superimposing into an unreadable result?
   Readable rests alone do not answer this. Record the exact interval,
   direction, sampled position and original image for any observed failure.

These observations are not new weighted scores. Report `pass`, `fail` or
`unobserved`, with evidence and coverage limits, alongside the unchanged
six visual criteria:

| Criterion | Observation retained |
|---|---|
| Backdrop | The object explains a work relationship or presents useful evidence, rather than only repeating labels. |
| Depth | Each of the five meanings develops the story and the ending answers the opening with supported limits. |
| Swap | The apparatus and behavior are specific to this work, not an unchanged generic scene with renamed text. |
| Detail and hierarchy | Important artifacts can be inspected at the captured size without obstructing readable copy or controls. |
| Spatial quality | One recognizable object persists; composition, movement and occlusion serve the explanation. Note changing-word defects here and under relevant detail observations. |
| Phone and still modes | Narrow views retain meaningful treatment; reduced-motion and failure modes retain complete accessible meanings. |

Keep visible ownership, illustrative/provenance labels, projected-versus-
observed evidence and sample/qualification limits as mandatory constraints.
Do not require arrows, a switch, additional controls, a fixed part count,
geometry, palette, printing technique or the accepted prototype's solution.
HTML may explain scheduling and repeat scene meaning for accessibility.
Accessible repetition is not failure. Do not require unsupported device
detail merely to make an artifact look richer.

## Original-image coverage, not a selected gallery

Use the coordinator capture for independent assessment; keep actor-owned
checks and image claims distinct. Before reviewing, verify the capture's
before/after source identity and its relation to the retained actor snapshot.
List every generated original image with path, SHA-256, mode, beat or
interval/control identity, and reviewer. Do not discard duplicates by hash:
different recorded positions remain separate evidence paths.

Inspection order prioritizes the question without reducing final coverage:

1. Open desktop and phone bench/evidence rests, then all five rests in
   desktop, phone and short-height modes. Check opening and ending together.
2. Open every recorded travel sample in each animated viewport, in interval
   order for forward travel and then reverse travel. Include all four
   adjacent-beat intervals and every sampled intermediate position, not
   only the two intervals that failed in the older study.
3. Open every reduced-motion still/rest image and all no-WebGL,
   no-JavaScript and context-loss originals. Actual context-loss event and
   scene state require the report; appearance alone cannot establish them.
4. Open all remaining original captures, including every control's before,
   focus/unfocused and after images and any supplemental originals retained
   by the coordinator. Note generated but incomplete attempt coverage
   separately if an attempt is interrupted.

**Every generated original from the assessed capture must be individually
opened at original resolution before claiming all-original review.** Contact
sheets may locate files but are not a substitute. Record which originals
were actually viewed. A missing, unreadable, hash-mismatched or ungenerated
image is an evidence gap, not a silent pass. Retain partial work without
calling it a complete review. All modes must have their report or be
explicitly unavailable before the final coverage statement.

## Separation, attribution and stopping

The text judge receives no image pixels and cannot establish legibility,
composition, clipping or visual quality. Its score remains a separate
artifact. Likewise, passing type/lint/build/control checks does not prove
that the scene explains the work, and a focus-only pointer exception does
not excuse an ordinary inaccessible control.

For each observed miss, distinguish actor implementation, engine behavior,
fixture constraint, measurement defect and missing evidence before suggesting
prose. Cite original paths and hashes, with source/report corroboration
where necessary. Do not infer that the actor skipped image review merely
because image-view calls are absent from the CLI stream. Do not fix an
actor's page during assessment or supply it review hints.

Record the baseline disposition before selecting any candidate text. If
both targeted observations pass, no redundant visual instruction or
candidate build follows without another specific supported question. If
only one fails, select only the corresponding justified change. Apply
these same criteria and complete image coverage to a permitted candidate;
do not tune expectations to its composition. A single pair does not
establish a general success rate or isolate the effect of each paragraph
when both change together.

Sampled travel is not every-frame or frame-rate proof. Software phone
viewports are not physical-phone use. No reviewed image, text score,
technical pass or earlier prototype approval is actual author approval of
these new outputs. Normal build activation, early journey, choice gates,
planning regression and the full six-stop non-sheet proof remain outside
this diagnostic. Stage 3 is not completed by this visual review.
