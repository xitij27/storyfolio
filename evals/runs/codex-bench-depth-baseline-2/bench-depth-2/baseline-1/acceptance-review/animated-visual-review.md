## Targeted findings

### 1. Inspectable bench relationship: fail, with partial successes

In all three bench rests, the board lifts away from the contact strip and
base. The visible gold contacts and separated support make fixture access
understandable. But the neighboring screen still says only BENCH and
OBSERVATION. Command, board response and the connection to observation are
not developed into an inspectable scene relationship. The HTML supplies
that chain. See [desktop bench](../acceptance/desktop/rest-bench.png),
[phone bench](../acceptance/mobile/rest-bench.png) and
[short bench](../acceptance/short/rest-bench.png).

This is not a finding that all geometry is decorative. The
[desktop evidence rest](../acceptance/desktop/rest-evidence.png) groups
CURRENT and INTERVAL above 2 YEARS, separate from BENCH RUN and 72 h.
That composition gives the two support paths spatial meaning. The copy
correctly explains measured current plus an assumed interval, the separate
observed run, and why 72 hours does not validate two years. Fixture access
and this grouping must retain credit when interpreting the baseline.
