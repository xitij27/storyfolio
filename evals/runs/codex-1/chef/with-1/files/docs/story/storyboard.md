## Idea
Question: **What happens before the menu is written?**

One continuous scroll follows six paper leaves from a ferment log. Their arrangements expose the evidence: influences, dates, log fields, work, measurements and the invitation to learn more. The menu comes last because Ines says it does. The conceptual answer is preparation, recording and checking before the weekly menu; final wording remains to be written and reviewed.

The log is a website interpretation, not a reproduction of an unseen notebook. No invented dates, salt ratios, recipes, handwriting, handwritten ticks or batch outcomes. No implication that a reader has learned safe fermentation from a visual diagram.

## Motion and access
Desktop uses one sticky scene beside ordinary HTML. Scroll gently rotates, spreads and gathers the same leaves. Settled states face the reader; no ongoing spinning, camera flight, custom cursor, parallax text or autoplay sound. Text remains readable during motion. The scene makes ordering, inspection and comparison visible; it does not gate content.

| Where | Experience |
|---|---|
| Phone | Single column; compact static/briefly transitioning paper assembly above chapter text; no full-screen sticky trap. Chronology becomes vertical, evidence grid becomes stacked cards, figures remain a readable pair. No hover interactions; controls at least 44 px. Small phones default to static evidence figures. |
| Reduced motion | No scroll-linked transforms or animated transitions. Static arrangement for each chapter preserves chronology, field order, gallery and comparison. |
| No WebGL | Responsive still images/HTML paper panels at the same chapter positions; all evidence, links and contacts remain. |
| No JavaScript | Server-rendered document with headings, images, native disclosure controls, anchor links and contact links. No loading overlay. |

Semantic headings, skip link, visible focus, sufficient contrast, image descriptions and zoom/reflow support. Duplicate canvas text is hidden from assistive technology; the HTML contains the full information. Image detail dialog, if built, manages focus and Escape correctly. Do not rely on colour or movement for meaning.

## Colour, material and type
Default selected by assistant: **Log paper** — warm paper #F2EBDD, ink #252A24, dark olive accent #46563D. Leaves are matte off-white with faint rules, restrained grain and soft edge shadows; no manufactured stains or false handwriting. Light and texture support legibility.

Alternative studies for a future build, not extra concepts: **Larder label** — ground #E8E8DE, ink #202923, accent #345D55, smooth pale label stock; **Clay and paper** — ground #F3E8DC, ink #352B27, accent #8B4636, thicker cream leaves. Pot colours are unverified; the clay palette is a design option, not sampled from an unseen photograph. Default is Log paper; alternatives can later be compared in scroll, without adding a public theme picker.

Two self-hosted open-source typefaces: **Source Sans 3** for headings, prose and controls; **IBM Plex Mono** for dates, field labels and measured values on the leaves. Comfortable 18–20 px body copy, roughly 60-character lines; large quiet headings rather than screen-filling slogans. Font licences and contrast to be checked during implementation. Meaningful faces have matching readable HTML; no requirement to zoom into a 3D label.

## Performance and acceptance for a future build
HTML first; scene loads after readable content. One shared geometry/material set, compressed responsive textures, no physics or continuous idle rendering. Mobile device-pixel-ratio cap; free resources when scene is inactive. Avoid expensive layered transparency. Initial mobile content target under 500 KB compressed excluding deferred 3D; desktop scene budget approximately 1.5 MB. If assets exceed budgets, simplify rather than delay the page.

Check keyboard-only use, reduced motion, JavaScript off, WebGL failure, 320 px width and real touch interaction. Target LCP under 2.5 s, CLS below 0.1, INP under 200 ms on a representative mobile connection; these are implementation targets, not measured results. Verify exact dates, measurement context, quote attribution and asset permissions before launch. Ensure obscured/cropped private content is absent from delivered files, not merely hidden with CSS.
