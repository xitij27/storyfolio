**Colour and type**

I chose warm log paper **#F2EBDD**, dark ink **#252A24**, and olive **#46563D**. The leaves have subtle rules, matte surfaces and soft shadows.

**Source Sans 3** handles readable text and headings; **IBM Plex Mono** handles dates, log fields and measurements. No imitation handwriting or manufactured stains. Two alternative palette studies are documented for later comparison.

**On a phone**

The site becomes a single readable column. Dates run vertically; evidence becomes stacked cards. Small phones use static chapter illustrations, with no prolonged pinned scene or compulsory animation.

Reduced-motion mode removes the moving transitions. Without WebGL or JavaScript, visitors still get the complete text, evidence, navigation and contact links. The 3D never controls access to information.
