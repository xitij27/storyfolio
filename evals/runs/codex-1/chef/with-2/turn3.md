The direction is **The ferment log**, built around one question: **What happens before the menu is written?**

The complete plan is saved in [storyboard.md](/tmp/storyfolio-evals/codex-chef-with-0bOu0J/docs/story/storyboard.md). Nothing has been built. This planning phase ends with the specification below.

**The idea and the continuous object**

Visitors follow the work that precedes your menu: preparation, recording, checking, readiness and teaching. The menu appears at the end because that is where it belongs in your actual working week.

One object carries the entire experience: **six paper leaves of a ferment log**. As visitors scroll, the same leaves open, separate into fields, become a photographic display, present evidence and return to one document.

Six is a design choice, not a claim about your actual log. The representation uses your field names and confirmed material; it will not contain invented recipes, salt percentages, batch dates or handwriting.

On desktop, the log occupies a large, sticky area beside readable text. The full sequence takes roughly nine to ten screen heights, with ordinary scrolling and no forced stops. All six leaves remain present, sometimes gathered behind the active one.

**1. Started — arrival**

The first screen establishes:

- Ines Okafor.
- Head Chef at Saltmarsh, Leeds.
- Your focus on fermentation, whole-vegetable cooking and apprentices.
- The question that guides the page.
- A compact piece of evidence: the whole kitchen’s reduction from 14 to 5 kg of waste per week, with its one-month 2023 measurement scope alongside it.

The log begins open, slightly staggered, with enough depth to feel physical. Its first leaf introduces your influences: your grandmother, *The Art of Fermentation* and *Wild Fermentation*. Your photograph of the ogiri pot can sit here once supplied. The opening does not invent a family story around it.

**What moves:** the leaves gently separate as the visitor begins scrolling, revealing the document’s structure.

**What visitors can click:** direct routes to workshops, apprentice enquiries and writer enquiries; chapter navigation; and a skip-to-contact link. Nobody needs to complete the animation to reach you.

**2. Started — background**

The second part of this chapter gives a short, accurate account of your background rather than a full employment-history section.

It includes the ten-week unpaid stage at Fjaerland: washing jars, labelling them and helping with two batches of garum. Your Saltmarsh dates distinguish acting head chef from March 2022 and Head Chef from September 2022.

**What moves:** three leaves form a compact sequence of influences and dated experience. The influence leaf stays visually separate from the timeline, avoiding an invented chronology.

**What visitors can click:** an accessible “Background” disclosure for the additional details. Essential identity and role information remain visible without opening it.

**3. Salt — how the work is recorded**

This chapter makes the ferment log understandable through its actual fields:

- Date started.
- Salt.
- Days checked.
- Ready.

The prep list appears alongside it as the other document you work from. This section explains the structure of your records without pretending to reproduce a real batch.

**What moves:** four leaves align into fields, a fifth holds the prep-list reference, and the sixth remains gathered at the edge. The arrangement makes the relationship between the fields visible.

**What visitors can click:** each field can be selected or focused to highlight its corresponding explanation. All explanations also remain readable in the page; this interaction is optional.

**4. Checks — practice, apprentices and writing**

The narrative widens from the record itself to your apprentice programme and *Brine*.

The content establishes that you run the programme and write about preserving. It does not invent a curriculum, apprentice outcomes or current vacancies. *Brine* appears through its title and confirmed subject matter; scans containing Olu’s drawings stay out until he agrees.

**What moves:** the six leaves pair into three working spreads: checking, apprentices and writing. Their arrangement connects these activities without claiming that your teaching follows a particular unconfirmed method.

**What visitors can click:** short text details about the programme and *Brine*, plus an apprentice-enquiry link. A publication link can be added if a real destination is available.

**5. Ready — work in view**

This is the most photographic part of the page.

Your own larder photographs provide the main material. A wider view establishes the space; a detail can show labels or texture after checking for sensitive information. Text entries alongside the photographs cover the larder, apprentice programme, *Brine* and workshops.

This is a work index, not six invented projects. If only a few photographs are suitable, the remaining leaves carry type.

**What moves:** the leaves spread into a loose contact sheet. The active image becomes larger while the others remain visible. There is no separate animated jar wall or cupboard scene.

**What visitors can click:** photographs open at a useful size, with credits and descriptions. The image viewer supports keyboard controls, Escape to close and return to the originating image. A workshop link jumps directly to the closing contact section.

Workshop dates, venue, booking details and availability will come from current information before launch.

**6. Review — the measured result**

The evidence gets a quiet, readable moment:

| Measure | Treatment |
|---|---|
| 14 kg → 5 kg per week | Show the raw figures clearly |
| Attribution | Whole kitchen; use “we” |
| Measurement | Weighed over one month in 2023 |
| Included | Prep and plate waste |
| Excluded | Bones, because they went to stock |

The page will not extrapolate annual savings, imply that this is a current measurement, or attribute the result solely to you or the ferment log.

**What moves:** two leaves square themselves towards the visitor, displaying the two values at equal typographic scale. The remaining leaves stay gathered nearby. Numbers do not count up or down.

**What visitors can click:** no interaction is required to understand the result. Its measurement note remains visible rather than hidden behind a tooltip.

**7. Review — Callum’s words**

The next settled moment presents the approved quotation:

> “Ines turned our bins into a larder. The best thing on the menu most weeks started as something we used to throw away.”

Attribution: Callum Reyes, owner of Saltmarsh.

**What moves:** the quotation leaf comes forward, with the measurement leaves still visible behind it.

**What visitors can click:** the normal chapter and contact navigation. There is no testimonial carousel or additional interaction to learn.

**8. Next batch — the menu and the invitation**

The ending completes the question from the opening: preparation and recorded checks come before readiness, and the menu is written last.

The final leaf introduces the menu’s place in that sequence. It does not display invented dishes.

**What moves:** the leaves gather back into one open log. The final leaf turns once and rests. The scene is still while visitors decide what to do.

**What visitors can click:**

1. **Workshops:** booking through your real booking destination, or an enquiry route until one exists.
2. **Apprenticeships:** an enquiry route without implying that recruitment is currently open.
3. **Writing enquiries:** a clear contact route for food writers.

The footer provides credits, relevant privacy information and a return-to-start link.

**The visual design**

The chosen look is warm working paper with precise printed information.

| Element | Choice |
|---|---|
| Page ground | Warm paper, `#F2EBDD` |
| Main ink | Near-black, `#242821` |
| Accent | Dark olive, `#495B38` |
| Log leaves | Almost-white, `#FFFCF4` |
| Surface | Fine rules, restrained grain, shallow shadows |

**Source Serif 4** handles headings and reading text. **IBM Plex Mono** handles log fields, dates, measurements and small navigation labels. Both can be self-hosted.

The layout gives prose comfortable line lengths and generous space. Important information never depends on reading tiny lettering on a tilted 3D surface. Photographs retain their own character rather than receiving a heavy colour treatment.

Movement consists of modest turns, shifts and regrouping. Each arrangement settles before its text needs attention. There is no automatic sound, cursor-following camera or scroll hijacking.

**Navigation and interaction throughout**

A discreet desktop rail names the six chapters: Started, Salt, Checks, Ready, Review and Next batch. On phones, this becomes a compact chapter menu.

Chapter links use ordinary page anchors. Browser back, keyboard navigation and direct links to sections work normally. Hover can emphasise an element, but never reveals information unavailable by touch or keyboard.

**On a phone and without 3D**

| Situation | What the visitor gets |
|---|---|
| **Phone** | The same complete story, using static two-dimensional log leaves above the text. Photographs stack naturally, contact buttons are large, and sections take only the space their content needs. |
| **Reduced motion** | Settled compositions with no leaf turns, camera movement, parallax or animated numbers. |
| **No WebGL, or graphics failure** | Static log illustrations replace the scene. Text, photographs, links and scroll position remain usable. |
| **No JavaScript** | Complete HTML content, static illustrations, ordinary chapter links and working contact destinations. |
| **Screen reader** | A properly ordered document with headings, image descriptions and every fact represented in text. The duplicate canvas scene is ignored. |

The default phone experience does not load the 3D engine. This keeps the page lighter and avoids compressing a desktop animation into a small screen.

The future implementation should support visible keyboard focus, strong contrast, zoom, touch targets of at least 44 pixels and no colour-only meanings.
