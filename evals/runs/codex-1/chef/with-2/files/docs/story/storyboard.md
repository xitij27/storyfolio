## Direction

Question: **What happens before the menu is written?**

One continuous scroll follows six leaves of a ferment log. The log's confirmed fields provide the sequence: started, salt, checks, readiness. Review and Next batch are editorial extensions, not claimed real headings. The ending answers through the order of the evidence: prep and recorded checks precede readiness, and the menu is written last. This is an editorial arrangement of confirmed facts, not a claim that the log caused the waste reduction.

The six leaves remain on screen throughout the enhanced desktop experience, even when five gather beside the active leaf. Paper geometry is simple. Faces contain meaningful words, source images or data; no independent spectacle, floating food, particle field, rotating chef emblem or fake kitchen simulation. Renderings are clearly designed representations, not counterfeit scans of her notebook. No made-up salt percentages, dates or food-safety guidance.

## Appearance

Selected default: **Working paper**. Warm paper ground #F2EBDD; near-black ink #242821; dark olive accent #495B38; leaves #FFFCF4. Fine ruled lines, restrained paper grain and believable shallow shadows. No artificial coffee stains, handwriting font or manufactured ageing. Olive is for links and focus details; text contrast must be checked during implementation.

Two self-hosted, open-license typefaces: **Source Serif 4** for headings and reading text; **IBM Plex Mono** for printed log fields, dates, measurements and small navigation labels. Serif provides a publishing voice for Ines's writing; mono gives the log orderly, readable columns. Use actual font weights, keep prose about 60–68 characters wide, body at least 18px where space permits, and avoid all-caps paragraphs. On phones the identity and question scale down fluidly. No important text exists only in a texture.

## Motion and interaction

Native vertical scrolling drives modest leaf translation, rotation and regrouping. No scroll hijacking, forced pauses, continuous idle spinning, automatic sound or pointer-chasing camera. Desktop uses a sticky scene with adjacent HTML text; exits from sticky regions must work with browser history, anchors and keyboard navigation. A leaf settles before the next block needs reading. Hover can emphasize a face but exposes no unique content. Clicking a work entry opens standard accessible content, never requires rotating an object.

| Context | Experience and substitute for 3D |
|---|---|
| Phone | Default static two-dimensional leaves above each text section, maintaining the same six-chapter order. No pinned canvas or multi-screen empty scroll. Stacked photo figures, a compact chapter menu, large contact buttons, minimum 44px touch targets. Result columns stack if needed. All work and proof remain. |
| Reduced motion | Static settled compositions per chapter; immediate navigation and no parallax, camera travel, leaf turning or animated number counting. |
| No WebGL / context lost | Replace canvas with prepared stills or CSS/HTML leaf arrangements; preserve scroll position and all links. No error dialog required to read. |
| No JavaScript | Server-rendered or static HTML with the complete chapter text, images, native anchors and contact destinations; static log illustrations. Native detail elements may hold optional background. |

Canvas is decorative to assistive technology because an equivalent ordered semantic document carries every fact, image description and relationship. Provide skip link, logical heading order, visible keyboard focus, alt text, sufficient contrast, and no colour-only meanings. Mobile layouts must survive zoom and long text.

Performance plan for future implementation: load readable HTML and the lead still first; lazy-load desktop 3D after primary content, load only needed font subsets, use compressed responsive images, cap canvas pixel density, and stop rendering offscreen. Reuse six simple sheet meshes and a small texture atlas; no large restaurant model. Aim for LCP under 2.5 seconds and CLS under 0.1 on representative mobile testing, with no 3D bundle in the default mobile path. These are targets, not measured results.
