**Colour, type and movement**

Keep the warm paper background (`#F4F0E7`), dark ink (`#172D32`) and deep teal (`#246A67`). Use clay (`#B5684E`) sparingly for annotations. Avoid borrowing early warning score colours for decoration: those colours have clinical meaning.

Use **Source Serif 4** for your point of view and major headings, with **Source Sans 3** for evidence, documents and navigation. Fine rules, aligned columns and generous spacing can echo clinical documentation without making the site resemble a hospital application.

Concentrate the motion in two moments: the opening document settling into place and the scanner-placement explanation. Elsewhere, use restrained transitions. The early warning chart and care plan influence the layout; they don’t need to become floating props or fabricated clinical records.

**Phone and accessibility**
