# A fixture that makes firmware observable

11 October 2026. Implements the authorized first step of the
[visual recovery plan](../plans/2026-10-11-visual-quality-recovery.md).
[Author authorization](../../history/2026-10-11-visual-prototype-approval.md).

## Question

Can the same illustrative apparatus connect Ada's firmware decisions to a
bench observation through useful spatial relationships and inspectable
surfaces? The previous repeated strips did not express that connection.

The object is one bring-up assembly, composed of a command console, generic
board, fixture cradle and observation instrument. Their forms are authored
illustrations. Physical details provide identity and depth, not a claim
about the appearance, circuitry or wiring of Mosslink.

1. Opening: assemble the four parts around a persistent cradle. Introduce
   the question of connecting code to something observable on the bench.
2. Middle: separate the same parts into a directed explanatory relationship:
   firmware command, generic board, fixture, bench observation. Four real
   HTML controls highlight each part and explain its role and ownership.
   Connections move with the parts and explain relationships, not wiring.
3. Ending: bring the assembly together, retaining the visible observation
   unit. Resolve the opening through Ada's supported contribution. Give the
   72-hour longest run and two-year projection distinct textual regions.
   They are different kinds of evidence, never a shared scale or morph.

## Evidence and limits

Use the frozen [facts](../../../evals/builds/embedded-1/workspace/docs/story/facts.md)
and [permitted explanatory drawing](../../../evals/builds/embedded-1/workspace/public/assets/bring-up-fixture.svg).
Ada wrote firmware, scheduled power states and built the fixture. A
colleague designed the PCB and analogue front end. No trace, actual circuit,
component layout, measurement values or hardware photograph is supplied.
There is no supported fix or outcome for the radio-awake incident.

The two-year battery projection uses bench current readings and an assumed
reporting cycle. The longest run was 72 hours. Six of forty engineering
samples were bench tested; none went to customers. One preliminary emissions
check passed and full qualification is unfinished. Keep these bounds in HTML.

## Visual treatment and acceptance

Warm pale ground, graphite typography, sage and ivory equipment, restrained
rust and gold accents. Use actual thickness, bevels, recessed surfaces and
small fasteners, distinct face hierarchies and large readable exhibit areas.
No external raster asset or downloaded model is needed for these schematic
forms. The engine's custom Piece, formation, face and lazy-extra hooks are
the implementation starting point.

Desktop places the object beside the narrative. Phone retains a compact
scene above shorter copy; still and failure modes retain authored vector
explanations. Review forward and backward transitions, control highlights,
phone framing, reduced motion, no WebGL, no JavaScript and context loss.
Save source hashes, capture conditions and evidence under
`evals/runs/visual-recovery-prototype-1/`. Visual review addresses Backdrop,
Depth, Swap, detail/hierarchy, spatial quality and phone/still presentation.
Technical checks cannot supply the author's visual approval.
