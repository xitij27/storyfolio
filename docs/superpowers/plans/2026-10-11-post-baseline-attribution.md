whether deleting the scene loses meaning. Do not append another generic
Backdrop slogan. If the final visual review confirms the implementation
miss, any candidate should be limited to carrying that existing obligation
through build review, with the saved failing scene as its rationale.
Do not mandate arrows, switches, the accepted geometry, piece count, palette
or another person's design. Scheduling may be explained in HTML; accessible
repetition is not a failure. The specific question is whether the apparatus
itself explains the bench relationship and evidence distinction.

Do not ship a build entry merely to attach that candidate. This diagnostic
explicitly overrides the planning-only endpoint, so normal activation and
scope remain untested. A candidate, its placement, changed input/measurement
identity and smallest useful comparison must be proposed together before
the separate paid-run decision. No exact new behavioral instruction is
applied by this plan, and no early-journey or full-batch run is authorized.

The early-journey case becomes the next workflow question only after this
quality issue is resolved or explicitly dispositioned. Before-stop-1 checks,
three sketches, three implemented looks, three whole-beat wording versions,
optional recommendation/refusal, planning-only regression, whole-site proof
and packaging retain their original gates. A source-level pass cannot
substitute for them.
