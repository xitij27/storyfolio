# Author's first reaction to the turn-6 site, 22 October 2026

Root (Claude Code) served a copy of the turn-6 site
(`journey-recovery-time-6-6k01cZ/site`, its existing `.next` build) on
127.0.0.1:3000 for the author's desktop review, after the whole-site check
([verdict](../../evals/runs/stage-3-spine-time-1/recovery/final-1-disposition.md))
had passed it technically and failed it on Detail and hierarchy. The
author's message after looking at it, verbatim:

> it looks ass tho. It is just the same 3d asset roaming around across each scene. Is this how you tell a story?

## What root found on reading it against the design

The design ([spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-portfolio-skill-design.md),
"What the object becomes") asks that each chapter give the pieces "one new
form of the same pieces that shows what the chapter is about": a map, a
diagram, an exhibit, a wall whose faces carry real pictures. The author
saw pieces moving between arrangements that do not show anything. The
whole-site check had already found that only the four largest words on the
pieces are readable, so the faces carry no content and the formations
cannot say what a chapter is about; the story is in the captions alone and
the 3D is decoration. The visual criteria that passed (Backdrop, Depth,
Swap, Phone/still) measure motion and rendering, not whether a formation
means something, so the check did not catch this as a failure of the
concept.

This is a rejection at the author's review gate. Stage 3 does not close on
this site. No skill change, run or push follows from this record alone.
