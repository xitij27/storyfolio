# The author's review of person test 4's site, 31 October to 1 November 2026

Person test 4 ran all six turns on the one-world skill
([run](../../evals/runs/stage-3-person-4/README.md),
[root's final review](../../evals/runs/stage-3-person-4/final-review/README.md)).
Its site tells Dawit's story on the map: the agent chose the sheet at stop
2, and the test's fixed turn 3 answer switched the whole site to the map.
Root served it at http://172.22.209.33:3140/ and asked for the author's
review. The author answered, verbatim:

> Moreover, it feels like there's no flow, is this a transition from
> Ethiopia to Netherlands? And just stuff happening over there, it does not
> feel rich in transitions. And it does not have a wow factor.

So Stage 3 does not close on this site.

## What root found behind it

After "where from", every chapter of Dawit's story stands in the
Netherlands: the hello sign, two of his three buildings, his tools, six
project cards and the contact card all stand around Rotterdam, and only
the diploma and one building in Addis Ababa. The camera flies to Addis
Ababa once for "studied" and back, then hardly moves, while cards stand up
from the ground. Each chapter's change is a card rising where the last
stood, not one picture becoming the next. The map's joins are a camera
tour; they are rich only when a person's places are many and spread out.

## The same site on the sheet, for comparison

Root built the finished site again with only `content/mix.ts` changed to
`WHOLE.sheet` (the world the agent had chosen), served it at
http://172.22.209.33:3141/ (from `/var/tmp/p4-final-sheet`), and shot its
moves ([frames](../../evals/runs/stage-3-person-4/final-review/moves-desktop-sheet.jpg)):
one sheet that curls into a globe, flattens into the diploma, curves into
the timeline, folds into the leaflet, tiles into the wall of six projects
and comes back whole as the contact page. Root asked the author whether
that flows, and what "wow" means to them, with a site or two that has it.

## The author's answer

> cool transitions mean wow. yes the sheet version is definitely better
> although it still doesnt feel there yet

So the work goes on from the sheet: its transitions are what must become
cool. The author's earlier direction stands: they judge examples rather
than answer technical questions ("come up with those stuff and then I can
provide my opinion", [27 October](2026-10-27-transition-examples-request.md)).
