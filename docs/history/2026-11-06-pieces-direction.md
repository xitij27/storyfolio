# The pieces: the author's direction, 6 November 2026

After the gallery became the front page and the older pieces moved to
https://xitij27.github.io/storyfolio/pieces/ as catalogue rows, the author
wrote, verbatim:

> https://xitij27.github.io/storyfolio/pieces/
> The pieces are just mentioned as text. I mean, come on, like this is
> supposed to be a front-end, a website, right? So how do you expect people
> to understand what they want just from text? You need to have cards where
> you show what the effect looks like, and maybe in the form of like,
> hopefully it animates on the website, or it shows the thing upon opening
> it up. So also you need to ensure that the individual pieces don't look
> like they're generated by AI as well. Use the skills which you have for
> detecting that and fix it.

Root read this as: the pieces index shows each piece as a card with a
picture of it that moves; a piece's page opens on the piece itself; and
each piece is audited with Hallmark and the frontend-design skill and its
generated-page tells fixed. What was done is recorded in
[the review](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-site-and-readme-review.md#the-pieces-shown-and-cleaned-6-november).
