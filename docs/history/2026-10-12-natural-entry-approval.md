# Single natural build-entry test approved

12 October 2026. The [exact proposed question](2026-10-12-natural-entry-request.md)
asked permission to prepare and run one agent turn, maximum 15 minutes,
followed by one browser verification, maximum 15 minutes. It excluded
a paid judge, retry and continuation, and said that any skill correction
and matched rerun would require a separate decision. Time limits were
explicitly not a token-cost cap.

The author answered through that question:

> Approve this single build-entry test

This authorizes the proposed unchanged-skill baseline and its preparation,
not a candidate correction, matched rerun, completed Stage 3, another attempt
at either interrupted capture, deployment or push. Use a fresh fictional
`bench-entry-1` derivative and `codex-bench-entry-baseline-1` cohort. Preserve
all historical inputs and evidence. Stop after one actor turn, including
failure or timeout, and at most one relevant post-turn browser capture.
If no new runnable implementation exists, record that result rather than
treating the stock starter as a delivered portfolio or spending a capture
that cannot answer the question.

The [approved protocol proposal](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-12-stage-3-natural-entry-proposal.md)
sets the narrow ordinary-continuation, runnable-spine and real-look-choice
observations. The [new work record](../../evals/runs/stage-3-natural-entry-1/findings.md)
will retain preparation, actual process outcomes, source and image evidence,
access/usage audit and the disposition. The main skill stays unchanged.
